% --- DATELINE & BODY CONTENT ---
\textbf{NAIROBI, Kenya — September 2026} — Muncheez Technologies Inc. today officially announced the release of its unified software marketplace and logistics infrastructure platform. Developed to solve critical bottlenecks in supply chain visibility and last-mile fulfillment, the system provides high-speed operational tools for merchants, logistics providers, and end consumers.

The platform integrates real-time marketplace discovery directly with end-to-end dispatch and tracking systems. By uniting merchant order management with direct logistics routing, Muncheez eliminates traditional operational delays and increases delivery precision for growing commercial enterprises.

``Our objective with Muncheez is to build a resilient, scalable technology backbone for modern trade,'' said the executive team at Muncheez. ``We are focusing entirely on reducing operational friction so that regional businesses can expand efficiently while giving customers complete transparency over their orders.''

The company will showcase the platform during an invitation-only debut hosted at Hackhouse Africa (124 Manyani East Road, Nairobi), bringing together key technology stakeholders, industry partners, and ecosystem leaders.
