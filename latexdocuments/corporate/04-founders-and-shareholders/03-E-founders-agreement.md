## OPERATIVE TERMS

### 1. Definitions

In this Agreement, capitalised terms have the meanings given in this Clause 1, in the Recitals, or in the Articles.

- **"Articles"** — the Articles of Association of the Company from time to time.
- **"Board"** — the board of Directors of the Company.
- **"Change of Control"** — a sale, transfer, or other disposal of more than 50% of the issued share capital of the Company to a third party, or any merger, consolidation, or other transaction having substantially the same effect.
- **"Confidential Information"** — has the meaning given in Clause 10.
- **"Founder IP"** — all intellectual property created, developed, or contributed by either Founder (whether alone or jointly with others) in connection with the business of the Company, whether before or after the date of this Agreement.
- **"Good Leaver"** — a Founder who ceases to be a Director, employee, or consultant of the Company for a reason described in Clause 8.2.
- **"Bad Leaver"** — a Founder who ceases to be a Director, employee, or consultant of the Company for a reason described in Clause 8.3.
- **"Reserved Matter"** — a matter listed in Clause 12.
- **"Subsidiary"** — has the meaning given in the Act.

### 2. Founders' Roles

2.1 Dean is appointed as **Founder & Chief Executive Officer** of the Company. Dean is responsible for the overall commercial leadership, business development, fundraising, partnerships, and external representation of the Company.

2.2 Kris is appointed as **Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer** of the Company. Kris is responsible for the technology vision, platform architecture, engineering leadership, source-code integrity, and technical compliance of the Company.

2.3 Each Founder will devote the necessary time, attention, and skill to discharge their respective responsibilities faithfully and in the best interests of the Company.

2.4 The role allocation above is a statement of operational focus. It does not constrain the Board's authority to allocate responsibilities differently by Board resolution.

### 3. Equity

3.1 The Founders' Equity is set out in Schedule 1 (currently drafted so that the exact figures are filled in by the Founders at the same time as the Articles and Memorandum; the underlying allocation logic should follow the proportions the Founders have agreed).

3.2 Any future issuance of shares or options is a **Reserved Matter** under Clause 12.

### 4. Vesting

4.1 All shares held by each Founder are subject to a **four (4) year vesting schedule** measured from the **Vesting Commencement Date** (defined below), with a **one (1) year cliff** (the "Vesting Terms").

4.2 The **Vesting Commencement Date** is the date of incorporation of the Company, unless the Founders agree in writing to a different commencement date.

4.3 Vesting operates as follows:

- (a) **Twenty-five per cent (25%)** of each Founder's shares vest on the **first anniversary** of the Vesting Commencement Date (the "Cliff").
- (b) The remaining **seventy-five per cent (75%)** vest in equal **monthly instalments** over the following **thirty-six (36) months** of continuous service as a Director, employee, or consultant of the Company.

4.4 If a Founder ceases to be a Director, employee, or consultant of the Company before the Cliff is reached, **all** of that Founder's unvested shares are forfeited (subject to Clause 8). If the Cliff has been reached, only the **unvested** portion is forfeited.

4.5 Vesting is suspended during any period the relevant Founder is on extended leave exceeding **three (3) months**, unless the Board otherwise resolves.

### 5. Capital Contributions

5.1 Each Founder shall contribute to the Company the cash, services, intellectual property, or other assets that they have agreed to contribute as set out in Schedule 2.

5.2 Any additional capital contributions by a Founder require Board approval and (if required by the Articles or the Act) shareholder approval.

### 6. Founder Services and IP

6.1 Each Founder shall use their reasonable endeavours to develop the business of the Company and to create, document, and assign to the Company all Founder IP.

6.2 Each Founder hereby:

- (a) assigns absolutely to the Company, with full title guarantee, all of their present and future right, title, and interest (including all intellectual property rights) in and to all Founder IP;
- (b) waives, in favour of the Company, all moral rights in respect of Founder IP to the extent permitted by applicable law;
- (c) undertakes to execute such documents and do such further acts as the Company may reasonably require to perfect the assignment in (a) and the waiver in (b); and
- (d) warrants that the Founder IP does not, to the best of their knowledge, infringe the rights of any third party.

6.3 Each Founder's obligations under this Clause 6 are conditional on the Company continuing to maintain each Founder's executive role and the protections set out in the Articles and any shareholders' agreement.

### 7. Company Opportunities

7.1 Each Founder shall promptly disclose to the Board any business opportunity that is or could reasonably be considered to be within the Company's current or reasonably foreseeable business.

7.2 Unless the Board resolves otherwise, a Founder may not personally pursue a Company opportunity without the prior written consent of the other Founder.

### 8. Departure and Leaver Classification

8.1 A Founder ceases to be a service-provider of the Company on the earliest of: (a) resignation; (b) removal as a Director; (c) termination of employment or consultancy; (d) death; or (e) permanent incapacity preventing performance.

8.2 A Founder is a **Good Leaver** if they cease to be a service-provider for any of the following reasons:

- (a) death or permanent incapacity;
- (b) illness or disability certified by a qualified medical practitioner that prevents continued service;
- (c) redundancy (in the employment-law sense) or termination by the Company other than for Cause;
- (d) constructive dismissal in the employment-law sense (where applicable);
- (e) any other reason that the remaining Founders and the Board (excluding the affected Founder) agree in writing is a Good Leaver reason.

8.3 A Founder is a **Bad Leaver** if they cease to be a service-provider for any of the following reasons:

- (a) dismissal for Cause (which includes gross misconduct, fraud, embezzlement, wilful breach of duty, breach of confidentiality, or final criminal conviction in a Kenyan court for an offence involving dishonesty);
- (b) voluntary resignation before the Cliff without the prior written consent of the other Founder;
- (c) breach of this Agreement that is not remedied within fourteen (14) days of written notice from the other Founder or the Company.

8.4 On a Good Leaver departure:

- (a) all vested shares remain the property of the departing Founder (or their estate);
- (b) the Company has a call option to purchase the vested shares at **Fair Market Value** (as defined below) within ninety (90) days of the departure; if the Company does not exercise the option, the departing Founder may transfer the shares in accordance with the Articles.

8.5 On a Bad Leaver departure:

- (a) all **unvested** shares are forfeited automatically;
- (b) the Company has a call option to purchase any **vested** shares at the **lower of** (i) Fair Market Value and (ii) the nominal value of the shares, within ninety (90) days of the departure.

8.6 "Fair Market Value" means the value of the relevant shares as agreed between the departing Founder (or their representative) and the Board, or failing agreement within thirty (30) days, as determined by an independent valuer appointed by the Company and approved by the departing Founder (or, failing agreement on the valuer, appointed by the Chairman for the time being of the ICPAK).

### 9. Death or Incapacity

9.1 On the death of a Founder, the personal representatives may elect, within twelve (12) months, either to (a) sell the vested shares to the Company at Fair Market Value, or (b) retain the vested shares (subject to the Articles and any shareholders' agreement).

9.2 On the permanent incapacity of a Founder, the Board may require the incapacitated Founder's representative to sell the vested shares to the Company at Fair Market Value.

### 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Each Founder undertakes that they shall not, at any time during or after the termination of this Agreement, disclose to any third party any Confidential Information of the Company, except:

- (a) with the prior written consent of the Company;
- (b) as required by law, court order, or a competent regulatory authority (and then only to the extent strictly required);
- (c) to professional advisers bound by an equivalent duty of confidentiality; or
- (d) information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Clause.

10.2 "Confidential Information" includes the Company's source code, algorithms, business plans, financial information, merchant data, rider data, customer data, partner terms, supplier terms, internal policies, and any other information that is identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would treat as confidential.

10.3 The confidentiality obligations in this Clause 10 survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**, except in respect of trade secrets, which remain confidential for so long as they retain trade-secret status.

### 11. Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation During Service

11.1 During the term of a Founder's service to the Company, the Founder shall not, without the prior written consent of the other Founder, directly or indirectly:

- (a) be engaged, concerned, or interested in any business that competes with the Company;
- (b) solicit, induce, or attempt to induce any employee, contractor, merchant, rider, or supplier of the Company to terminate that relationship; or
- (c) accept (or solicit the offer of) any order from a customer or merchant of the Company for the benefit of a competing business.

11.2 Clause 11.1 does not apply to a passive holding of less than 5% of the issued share capital of a publicly listed company.

11.3 Post-service non-compete and non-solicit obligations are subject to the limits in the Employment Act, 2007 (including s. 33) and the general doctrine of restraint of trade. The Parties intend any post-service restraint to be reasonable in scope, duration, and geography.

### 12. Decision-Making and Reserved Matters

12.1 Each Founder shall use their reasonable endeavours to make decisions of the Board and the shareholders unanimously where possible.

12.2 The following matters are **Reserved Matters** and require the prior written consent of **both** Founders:

- (a) any matter that is a Reserved Matter under the Articles;
- (b) any change to the role allocation between the Founders (Clauses 2.1 and 2.2);
- (c) the adoption or amendment of any Founders' equity, vesting, or leaver arrangement;
- (d) the issuance of any shares or options to any third party;
- (e) the admission of any new Founder;
- (f) any decision relating to the appointment or removal of the Auditor;
- (g) any decision to commence, settle, or abandon material litigation;
- (h) any decision to enter into any transaction with a Founder or with any person connected with a Founder;
- (i) any decision relating to the dividend or distribution policy.

### 13. Deadlock

13.1 If the Founders are unable to agree on a Reserved Matter within **fourteen (14) days** of the matter being raised, either Founder may invoke this Clause 13 by written notice to the other Founder (a **"Deadlock Notice"**).

13.2 Within fourteen (14) days of a Deadlock Notice, the Founders shall meet in person and use good-faith efforts to resolve the deadlock.

13.3 If the deadlock is not resolved within thirty (30) days of the Deadlock Notice, the Founders shall refer the dispute to mediation administered by the **Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch)** under its mediation rules in force at the time.

13.4 If mediation does not resolve the dispute within sixty (60) days of the Deadlock Notice, either Founder may invoke the buy-sell mechanism in Clause 13.5.

13.5 The buy-sell mechanism operates as follows:

- (a) the initiating Founder (the **"Offeror"**) serves a written offer to the other Founder (the **"Offeree"**) to either (i) buy all of the Offeree's shares at the price specified, or (ii) sell all of the Offeror's shares to the Offeree at the same price;
- (b) the Offeree must, within thirty (30) days, elect in writing which option they accept;
- (c) if the Offeree fails to elect within thirty (30) days, the Offeree is deemed to have elected to sell;
- (d) the price is the price specified by the Offeror in good faith, supported by such evidence as the Offeror considers appropriate.

13.6 The buy-sell mechanism in Clause 13.5 does not apply during the Cliff.

### 14. Information Rights

14.1 Each Founder is entitled, on reasonable notice, to:

- (a) inspect the books and records of the Company during normal business hours;
- (b) receive monthly management accounts within twenty-one (21) days of month-end;
- (c) receive quarterly business updates from the other Founder;
- (d) attend any board meeting and any shareholder meeting in their capacity as a Director or Shareholder.

### 15. Insurance and Indemnity

15.1 The Company shall consider, on a case-by-case basis, the purchase of directors' and officers' (D&O) liability insurance covering the Founders in their capacity as Directors.

15.2 Subject to the Act and the Articles, the Company shall indemnify each Founder against liabilities properly incurred in the course of their duties as a Director, to the maximum extent permitted by law.

### 16. Termination

16.1 This Agreement terminates on the earliest of:

- (a) the written agreement of the Parties;
- (b) a Change of Control;
- (c) the winding-up of the Company; or
- (d) such other event as the Parties may agree in writing.

16.2 Termination does not affect any rights or obligations that have accrued before termination, including the obligations in Clauses 6, 10, and 11.

### 17. General

17.1 **Governing law:** this Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya.
17.2 **Dispute resolution:** subject to Clause 13, any dispute shall be referred to mediation under the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch) mediation rules, and failing settlement, to binding arbitration in Nairobi under the Arbitration Act (Cap. 49 of the Laws of Kenya).
17.3 **Notices:** notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, registered post, or email to the addresses set out above.
17.4 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes any prior agreement.
17.5 **Variation:** no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by all Parties.
17.6 **Severability:** if any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
17.7 **Third party rights:** no third party has any right to enforce this Agreement.
17.8 **Counterparts:** this Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by Electronic Means.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Restraint of trade.** Clause 11 is drafted so that the during-service non-compete is broad but the post-service restraint is expressly limited by the Employment Act, 2007 and the doctrine of restraint of trade. The Advocate should confirm the current enforceability of post-employment restraints in Kenya and the limits of s. 33 of the Employment Act.
2. **IP assignment.** Clause 6 assigns present and future Founder IP. The Advocate should consider whether a separate Founder IP Assignment Agreement ([`05-A`](-10-ip/05-A-founder-ip-assignment.md:1)) should also be executed for evidentiary clarity.
3. **Buy-sell mechanism.** Clause 13.5 (the "Russian roulette" mechanism) is common but commercially aggressive. The Advocate should consider whether the Founders wish to soften the default to a third-party valuer rather than the Offeror's price.
4. **Leaver triggers.** The Good Leaver / Bad Leaver classifications in Clauses 8.2 and 8.3 should be reviewed against Kenyan employment-law concepts (including "Cause" in the employment context).
5. **Compatibility with the Articles.** Several Clauses (e.g. vesting, leaver triggers, information rights) will need to be consistent with the Articles. The Advocate should review both documents together.
6. **Mediation / arbitration.** Clause 17.2 names the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch) and the Arbitration Act (Cap. 49). The Advocate should confirm the current name and rules of the Chartered Institute and whether the seat of arbitration and the language of arbitration should be specified.
