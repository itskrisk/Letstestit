### 4. Voting

4.1 Subject to this Agreement, the Articles, and the Act, each Shareholder has one vote for each Share held.

4.2 Subject to Clause 9 (Reserved Matters), decisions of the Shareholders in a general meeting are taken by ordinary resolution.

### 5. Pre-emption on Issue of New Shares

5.1 Before the Company issues any new Shares (other than under Clause 11), the Company shall first offer those Shares to the existing Shareholders pro rata to their existing shareholdings, on the same terms.

5.2 The procedure for pre-emption in this Clause 5 mirrors the procedure in the Articles. The Shareholders shall procure that the Board implements the pre-emption in good time.

5.3 The pre-emption right does not apply to:

- (a) an issue of Shares under an employee share scheme approved by the Board;
- (b) an issue of Shares to a Permitted Transferee of an existing Shareholder; or
- (c) any issue that the Shareholders waive in writing.

### 6. Transfer Restrictions

6.1 Save as expressly permitted by this Clause 6, no Shareholder may transfer any Shares without the prior written consent of the other Shareholders.

6.2 **Permitted transfers.** A Shareholder may transfer Shares to a Permitted Transferee, provided that the Permitted Transferee first signs a Deed of Adherence.

6.3 **Right of First Refusal (ROFR).** If a Shareholder (the **"Selling Shareholder"**) receives a bona fide third-party offer to purchase Shares (the **"ROFR Offer"**), the Selling Shareholder must first offer those Shares to the other Shareholders (the **"Continuing Shareholders"**) on the same terms. The Continuing Shareholders have twenty-one (21) days to accept the offer in respect of all or part of their pro rata entitlement. Any Shares not accepted may be transferred to the third-party within sixty (60) days of the expiry of the offer period, on terms no more favourable than those offered to the Continuing Shareholders.

6.4 **Tag-along.** If a Shareholder proposes to transfer Shares (other than to a Permitted Transferee) representing more than five per cent (5%) of the issued share capital, the other Shareholders have the right to **tag along** — that is, to require the proposed transferee to acquire their Shares on the same terms pro rata.

6.5 **Drag-along.** If Shareholders holding at least **seventy-five per cent (75%)** of the issued Shares (the **"Drag Majority"**) propose to transfer their Shares to a third party on arm's length terms, they may **drag along** the remaining Shareholders — that is, require the remaining Shareholders to transfer their Shares on the same terms. The Drag Majority must act in good faith and the terms offered to the dragged Shareholders must be the same as those offered to the Drag Majority.

6.6 **Compulsory transfer on a Bad Leaver event.** If a Founder becomes a Bad Leaver under the Founders' Agreement, the Company has the compulsory-transfer rights in the Founders' Agreement, which the Shareholders procure are exercised.

### 7. Information Rights

7.1 The Company shall provide each Shareholder with:

- (a) annual audited (or, where the Company is exempt from audit, reviewed) accounts within one hundred and twenty (120) days of the end of the financial year;
- (b) quarterly management accounts within forty-five (45) days of quarter-end;
- (c) an annual budget for the next financial year, before the start of that financial year;
- (d) prompt notice of any material event affecting the Company.

### 8. Dividend Policy

8.1 The Board may declare dividends subject to the Reserved Matters and the Articles.

8.2 The Shareholders acknowledge that, in the early years of the Company, profits are likely to be reinvested and that dividends may be limited or nil.

### 9. Reserved Matters

9.1 The Company shall not, and the Shareholders shall procure that no subsidiary of the Company shall, take any of the following actions without the prior written consent of **Shareholders holding at least seventy-five per cent (75%)** of the issued Shares (the **"Supermajority Consent"**):

- (a) altering the Articles or this Agreement;
- (b) issuing, allotting, or agreeing to issue or allot any Shares or any instrument convertible into Shares (subject to Clause 11);
- (c) reducing the share capital, capital redemption reserve, or share premium account;
- (d) declaring or paying any dividend or other distribution;
- (e) acquiring or disposing of any business or material asset in any financial year for consideration exceeding [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend KES 1,000,000];
- (f) incurring any indebtedness or giving any guarantee, indemnity, or security for an aggregate amount exceeding [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend KES 1,000,000] in any financial year;
- (g) entering into, varying, or terminating any contract with a Shareholder, Director, or Founder, or with any person connected with any of them, other than on arm's length terms;
- (h) the appointment or removal of the Auditor;
- (i) the adoption or material amendment of the business plan or annual budget;
- (j) the adoption or amendment of any share option, share incentive, or employee share scheme;
- (k) any decision to commence, settle, or abandon any material litigation;
- (l) any change to the nature of the business or the commencement of any new line of business;
- (m) any decision to wind up the Company or to apply for the appointment of a liquidator or provisional liquidator.

### 10. Deadlock

10.1 If the Shareholders are unable to agree on a matter requiring Supermajority Consent within thirty (30) days of the matter being raised, either Shareholder may serve a Deadlock Notice.

10.2 The Parties shall follow the mediation and buy-sell procedure in Clause 13 of the Founders' Agreement, mutatis mutandis, with the same Offeror / Offeree mechanism.

10.3 The Parties acknowledge that the buy-sell mechanism in Clause 13.5 of the Founders' Agreement is a last-resort mechanism and that they will in good faith seek an amicable resolution before exercising it.

### 11. Investor Entry

11.1 When the Company proposes to admit a third-party investor, the Company and the existing Shareholders shall negotiate a Share Subscription Agreement and an Investor Shareholders' Agreement (or a Deed of Adherence to this Agreement) on terms that are customary for the relevant financing round.

11.2 The Company shall not, and the existing Shareholders shall procure that the Company shall not, issue any Shares to a third-party investor without Supermajority Consent.

11.3 Any new Shareholder must sign a Deed of Adherence before being entered in the Register of Members.

### 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each Shareholder shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the Company and shall not disclose it to any third party except:

- (a) with the prior written consent of the Company;
- (b) as required by law, court order, or competent regulatory authority;
- (c) to professional advisers bound by an equivalent duty of confidentiality; or
- (d) information that is or becomes generally available to the public other than through a breach of this Clause.

12.2 The obligations in this Clause 12 survive termination of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years**, except in respect of trade secrets, which remain confidential for so long as they retain trade-secret status.

### 13. Term and Termination

13.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date first written above and continues until the earliest of:

- (a) the written agreement of the Parties;
- (b) a Change of Control;
- (c) the winding-up of the Company; or
- (d) such other event as the Parties may agree in writing.

13.2 Termination does not affect any rights or obligations that have accrued before termination.

### 14. General

14.1 **Governing law:** the laws of the Republic of Kenya.
14.2 **Dispute resolution:** subject to Clause 10, any dispute shall be referred to mediation administered by the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch) under its mediation rules, and failing settlement, to binding arbitration in Nairobi under the Arbitration Act (Cap. 49 of the Laws of Kenya).
14.3 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement (together with the Articles) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter.
14.4 **Variation:** no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by all Parties.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Compatibility with the Act.** Pre-emption rights, transfer restrictions, tag-along and drag-along rights must be compatible with the Companies Act, 2015 and the Articles. Private-company share-transfer restrictions are valid, but they must be carefully drafted to be enforceable.
2. **Drag-along threshold.** Clause 6.5 sets the drag-along at 75%. Kenyan practice varies; the Advocate should confirm what threshold the Founders wish to set and whether it needs to align with the Founders' Agreement.
3. **Reserved Matter thresholds.** Schedule 2 thresholds should be calibrated to the size and stage of the Company. The Advocate should review against the Company's expected transaction volume.
4. **Investor accession.** Clause 11 is drafted to allow future investors to accede by Deed of Adherence. The Advocate should confirm the Deed of Adherence form in Schedule 3 reflects current practice and does not inadvertently allow an investor to bypass key protections.
5. **Mediation / arbitration.** Same flags as in the Founders' Agreement — confirm the current name and rules of the Chartered Institute and the seat / language of arbitration.
