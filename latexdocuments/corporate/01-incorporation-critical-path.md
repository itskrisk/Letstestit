## Hard "Do Not" list

- Do **not** request a CR12 before incorporation. It does not exist before then.
- Do **not** describe the Articles of Association as a "CR2" — CR2 is the Memorandum form in some contexts, but the Articles are a separate constitutional document.
- Do **not** file anything with BRS before reserving the name.
- Do **not** begin processing personal data before ODPC registration (or at minimum before a published Privacy Policy and lawful basis are in place).
- Do **not** begin onboarding merchants or riders before the relevant template agreements are finalised and signed.
- Do **not** classify riders as independent contractors simply because that is administratively cheaper — analyse the actual working relationship first.
