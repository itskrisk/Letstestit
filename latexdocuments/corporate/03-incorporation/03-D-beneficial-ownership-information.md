## ⚠️ Drafting note

This document is the data set required for the **Beneficial Ownership Information (BOI)** filing under the Companies (Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations and the Companies Act, 2015. It is a **pre-population template** for the BRS eCitizen BOI submission. The actual filing is done electronically on the BRS portal.

This document does **not** contain fabricated data. Every personal identifier is marked `[TO BE CONFIRMED]` until the relevant founder supplies it.

The current BOI threshold, the meaning of "beneficial owner," the disclosure fields, and the update cycle must be verified against the current BOI Regulations and any current BRS guidance before filing.

---

## DOCUMENT PURPOSE

Identifies every natural person who ultimately owns or controls the Company, either through direct shareholding or through other means of control. Required at incorporation and on each subsequent change in beneficial ownership.

## LEGAL BASIS

- **Companies Act, 2015** — provisions on beneficial ownership and the Register of Beneficial Owners.
- **Companies (Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations** — definitions, threshold, fields, and update obligations.
- **Business Registration Service** — current BRS eCitizen BOI portal requirements.

## WHO USES IT

- **Founders** — supply the data and sign the declarations.
- **Advocate / Filing Agent** — files via BRS eCitizen.
- **Company Secretary** — maintains the internal Beneficial Ownership Register.
- **BRS** — receives and maintains the BOI register.

## ONGOING OBLIGATIONS

The Company must:

1. Maintain an internal **Register of Beneficial Owners** reflecting the BOI data filed with BRS.
2. Update the BOI filing with BRS within the period prescribed by the BOI Regulations whenever there is a change in beneficial ownership (new beneficial owner, change in percentage, change in means of control, beneficial owner ceasing to be one).
3. Provide a copy of the BOI register to BRS on request.
4. Keep the BOI register available for inspection by competent authorities.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Current BOI Regulations.** The Advocate must confirm the current definition of "beneficial owner," the applicable threshold (commonly understood as 10% ownership or significant control, but verify the exact figure), and the required disclosure fields.
2. **Data Protection.** The BOI data is sensitive personal data. The Company must have a Data Protection Policy and Privacy Notice covering the storage, processing, and disclosure of BOI data, including lawful basis under the Data Protection Act, 2019.
3. **Update timeline.** Verify the current BOI update timeline (typically within a defined number of days of a change) and ensure the Company's internal governance triggers the update on time.
4. **Inspection rights.** Verify who has statutory rights to inspect the BOI register (BRS, law-enforcement agencies, etc.) and ensure the Company's Privacy Notice reflects this.
