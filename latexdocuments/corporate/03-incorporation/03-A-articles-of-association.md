## DOCUMENT PURPOSE

The constitutional document of the Company. It governs the internal affairs of the Company, the rights and obligations of its shareholders, the powers and duties of its directors, the issuance and transfer of shares, and the conduct of meetings and resolutions.

## PART I — PRELIMINARY

### 1. Interpretation

1.1 In these Articles, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Act"** means the Companies Act, 2015 of the Laws of Kenya, as amended from time to time.
- **"Articles"** means these Articles of Association as adopted or amended from time to time.
- **"Beneficial Owner"** has the meaning given in the Companies (Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations.
- **"Board"** means the board of Directors of the Company.
- **"Company"** means **Muncheez Technologies Limited**.
- **"Director"** means a director of the Company.
- **"Electronic Means"** has the meaning given in the Act and includes email, video conference, and any platform approved by the Board for written or electronic resolutions.
- **"Founder"** means, as at the date of incorporation, Dean Ndere and Kris Kamau, and thereafter any person admitted as a Founder by special resolution.
- **"Fully Diluted Basis"** means the calculation of share percentages assuming the conversion of all issued, unissued, options, and convertible instruments into Ordinary Shares.
- **"Member"** or **"Shareholder"** means a person entered in the Register of Members as a holder of shares.
- **"Model Articles"** means the Model Articles for Private Companies Limited by Shares prescribed under the Companies Act, 2015.
- **"Ordinary Shares"** means the single class of shares issued by the Company from time to time.
- **"Reserved Matter"** has the meaning given in Article 19.
- **"Seal"** means the common seal of the Company, if adopted.
- **"Secretary"** means any person appointed to perform the duties of the company secretary.

1.2 Words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa. Words importing one gender include all genders.

1.3 Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

### 2. Adoption and Effect of Model Articles

2.1 The Model Articles apply to the Company **save** to the extent that they are excluded, varied, or modified by these Articles. Where any provision of these Articles conflicts with the Model Articles, these Articles prevail.

2.2 In the event of ambiguity or conflict between these Articles and the Act, the Act prevails.

## PART II — SHARE CAPITAL AND SHARES

### 3. Authorised Share Capital

3.1 The authorised share capital of the Company is **KES [TO BE CONFIRMED] divided into [TO BE CONFIRMED] Ordinary Shares of KES [TO BE CONFIRMED] each** (or such number and nominal value as the Board may determine from time to time, subject to these Articles and the Act).

3.2 The Company may issue shares of a single class, being Ordinary Shares, unless and until the Board resolves (subject to these Articles and any shareholders' agreement in force) to create additional classes.

### 4. Rights Attaching to Ordinary Shares

4.1 Subject to any special rights attaching to any class of shares that may be created in the future, the Ordinary Shares confer on the holder:

- (a) the right to receive notice of, attend and vote at any general meeting of the Company (one vote per share held);
- (b) the right to receive dividends declared by the Company in accordance with these Articles and the Act; and
- (c) the right to participate in a distribution of capital on a winding up of the Company.

### 5. Allotment of Shares

5.1 Subject to the Act and these Articles, the Board may allot, grant options over, or otherwise dispose of any unissued shares to such persons, at such times, and on such terms as the Board determines.

5.2 No share may be allotted unless the allotment is authorised by the Board and (where required by the Act) by an ordinary resolution of the Members.

5.3 The Board shall ensure that, upon any allotment, the Company complies with the Act (including the issuing of share certificates, the maintenance of the Register of Members, and the filing of any return of allotment required by the Act).

### 6. Share Certificates

6.1 The Company shall issue a share certificate in respect of each allotment of shares, in accordance with the Act, within the period prescribed by the Act.

6.2 Every share certificate shall be signed by a Director and the Secretary (or by two Directors) and shall specify the name of the holder, the number and class of shares, and any distinctive numbers.

### 7. Lien on Shares

7.1 The Company shall have a first and paramount lien on every share (not being a fully paid share) for all monies (whether presently payable or not) called or payable at a fixed time in respect of that share.

7.2 The Company may sell any share on which the Company has a lien in the manner provided by the Act.

### 8. Calls on Shares

8.1 The Board may make calls on Members in respect of any monies unpaid on their shares (whether on account of the nominal value of the shares or by way of premium), provided that no call shall exceed the amount unpaid on the shares.

8.2 A call shall be deemed to have been made at the time when the resolution of the Board authorising the call was passed.

8.3 Joint holders of a share are jointly and severally liable to pay all calls in respect of it.

### 9. Forfeiture of Shares

9.1 If a Member fails to pay any call or instalment on or before the due date, the Board may serve a notice on the Member requiring payment and stating that, if payment is not made within the period specified (not being less than fourteen (14) days), the share in respect of which the call was made may be forfeited.

9.2 If the requirements of any such notice are not complied with, the share may be forfeited by a resolution of the Board. A forfeited share may be sold or otherwise disposed of as the Board determines.

### 10. Transfer of Shares

10.1 Shares are transferable in accordance with the Act and these Articles.

10.2 A transfer of shares shall be in the usual common form, or in such form as the Board may from time to time approve, and shall be signed by or on behalf of both the transferor and the transferee.

10.3 The Board may refuse to register a transfer of a share where:

- (a) the share is not fully paid and the unpaid amount has not been paid;
- (b) the Company has a lien over the share;
- (c) the transfer is not accompanied by such evidence as the Board reasonably requires to establish the identity of the transferee; or
- (d) registration would, in the opinion of the Board, breach any law, regulation, or contractual restriction binding on the Company (including any shareholders' agreement).

10.4 The Board shall not refuse to register a transfer for any other reason.

10.5 If the Board refuses to register a transfer, it shall notify the transferee and the transferor of the refusal and the reasons for it within the period prescribed by the Act.

### 11. Pre-emption Rights

11.1 Subject to the Act and any shareholders' agreement in force, before any share is allotted or transferred to a person who is not already a Member, the share shall first be offered to the existing Members in proportion to their existing shareholdings, on the same terms as those on which it is proposed to be allotted or transferred.

11.2 The procedure for pre-emption shall be:

- (a) the Board shall give written notice to each Member specifying the number of shares offered, the price, and the period (not less than twenty-one (21) days) within which the offer must be accepted;
- (b) each Member may accept the offer in respect of all or part of their entitlement;
- (c) any shares not accepted by Members within the period may, on the same terms, be allotted or transferred to the proposed allottee or transferee.

11.3 The pre-emption right in this Article does not apply to:

- (a) an allotment of shares pursuant to an employee share scheme approved by the Board;
- (b) a transfer to a Member's spouse, child, or a trust for the benefit of such persons, provided that the transferee first signs a deed of adherence to any shareholders' agreement in force; or
- (c) any allotment or transfer that the Members waive in writing.

### 12. Transmission of Shares

12.1 If a Member dies, the survivor or survivors (where the Member was a joint holder) or the personal representatives of the deceased (where the Member was a sole holder) shall be the only persons recognised by the Company as having any title to the deceased Member's shares.

12.2 A person becoming entitled to a share by transmission may, on producing such evidence as the Board reasonably requires, elect either to be registered as a Member or to transfer the share to a third party. If they elect to be registered, they shall give notice to the Company. If they elect to transfer, they shall execute a transfer in favour of the transferee.

### 13. Alteration of Share Capital

13.1 The Company may, in accordance with the Act and by ordinary resolution:

- (a) consolidate and divide all or any of its share capital into shares of a larger nominal value than its existing shares;
- (b) subdivide all or any of its share capital into shares of a smaller nominal value than its existing shares;
- (c) cancel any shares that, at the date of the resolution, have not been taken or agreed to be taken by any person, and diminish the amount of its share capital by the amount of the shares so cancelled; and
- (d) redenominate its share capital.

13.2 The Company may, by special resolution and in accordance with the Act, reduce its share capital, any capital redemption reserve, or any share premium account.

## PART III — SHAREHOLDERS AND MEETINGS

### 14. Register of Members

14.1 The Company shall maintain a Register of Members in accordance with the Act. The Register shall be available for inspection by Members in accordance with the Act.

14.2 The Company shall also maintain a Register of Beneficial Owners in accordance with the Beneficial Ownership Information Regulations.

### 15. General Meetings

15.1 The Board may convene a general meeting of the Company at any time. The Board shall convene an Annual General Meeting (AGM) in accordance with the Act, unless the Company passes a resolution in accordance with the Act to dispense with the holding of AGMs.

15.2 A general meeting may be called by Members in accordance with the Act.

15.3 Notice of every general meeting shall be given to every Member, every Director, the Secretary (if any), and the Auditor (if any), in accordance with the Act.

15.4 A general meeting may be held by Electronic Means, provided that all participants can communicate with each other and the meeting is held in a manner consistent with the Act.

### 16. Quorum

16.1 The quorum at a general meeting is **two Members present in person or by proxy and entitled to vote**, or such other number as may be required by the Act.

16.2 If a quorum is not present within thirty (30) minutes after the time appointed for the meeting, the meeting shall be dissolved (if convened on the requisition of Members) or adjourned to the same day in the next week (or as the Board may determine).

### 17. Voting

17.1 Subject to these Articles and the Act, every Member present in person or by proxy has one vote for each Ordinary Share held.

17.2 A resolution put to the vote at a general meeting shall be decided on a show of hands unless a poll is demanded in accordance with the Act.

17.3 A poll may be demanded by:

- (a) the chairperson of the meeting;
- (b) any Member present in person or by proxy and entitled to vote; or
- (c) any number of Members representing not less than one-tenth of the total voting rights of all the Members having the right to vote at the meeting.

17.4 On a poll, votes may be cast in person or by proxy.

### 18. Proxies

18.1 A Member may appoint a proxy to attend and vote at a general meeting on their behalf. The proxy shall be appointed by an instrument in writing signed by the Member (or their duly authorised attorney).

18.2 A proxy shall not be entitled to vote on a show of hands unless the proxy is a Member.

### 19. Reserved Matters

19.1 The Company shall not, and the Board shall procure that no subsidiary of the Company shall, take any of the following actions (each a **"Reserved Matter"**) without the prior approval of Members holding at least **seventy-five per cent (75%)** of the issued Ordinary Shares (a "Supermajority Resolution"):

- (a) altering the Articles or the Memorandum;
- (b) creating, issuing, or allotting any new shares or any instrument convertible into shares, other than under an employee share scheme approved by the Board;
- (c) reducing the share capital, capital redemption reserve, or share premium account;
- (d) declaring or paying any dividend or other distribution;
- (e) acquiring any asset or business, or disposing of any material asset or business of the Company, in each case for consideration exceeding [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend KES 1,000,000];
- (f) incurring any indebtedness or giving any guarantee, indemnity, or security in an aggregate amount exceeding [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend KES 1,000,000] in any financial year;
- (g) entering into, varying, or terminating any contract with a Director or Founder, or with any person connected with a Director or Founder, other than on arm's length terms;
- (h) appointing or removing the Auditor;
- (i) approving the annual accounts;
- (j) adopting or materially amending the business plan or annual budget;
- (k) changing the nature of the business of the Company or the commencement of any new line of business;
- (l) the appointment, removal, or material variation of the terms of service of any executive Director or officer of the Company;
- (m) the registration, prosecution, defence, abandonment, or settlement of any litigation, arbitration, or administrative proceeding involving an amount exceeding [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend KES 500,000];
- (n) the adoption or amendment of any share option, share incentive, or employee share scheme;
- (o) any decision to wind up the Company or to apply for the appointment of a liquidator or provisional liquidator.

19.2 The threshold for Reserved Matters may be amended only by special resolution.

### 20. Written Resolutions

20.1 Subject to the Act, a resolution in writing signed or approved by Electronic Means by Members holding the requisite majority (including any Supermajority Resolution) is as valid as if it had been passed at a general meeting duly convened and held.

20.2 Written resolutions may be executed in counterparts and circulated by email or via a secure electronic platform designated by the Secretary.

## PART IV — DIRECTORS

### 21. Number, Appointment and Removal

21.1 The minimum number of Directors is **two (2)**. The maximum number is **five (5)** unless the Members resolve otherwise by ordinary resolution.

21.2 The initial Directors are **Dean Ndere** (Founder & Chief Executive Officer) and **Kris Kamau** (Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer).

21.3 A Director may be appointed by ordinary resolution of the Members or by the Board, provided that any appointment by the Board is subsequently ratified by the Members at the next AGM.

21.4 A Director may be removed by ordinary resolution of the Members in accordance with the Act.

21.5 A Director vacates office if they:

- (a) resign by written notice to the Company;
- (b) are removed by ordinary resolution of the Members;
- (c) become disqualified from acting as a director under the Act or any other applicable law;
- (d) become bankrupt or make any arrangement with their creditors generally;
- (e) become of unsound mind; or
- (f) have been absent from meetings of the Board for a continuous period of six (6) months without special leave of absence from the Board and the Board resolves that they vacate office.

### 22. Directors' Powers and Duties

22.1 The Board is responsible for the management of the business of the Company and may exercise all powers of the Company that are not required by the Act or these Articles to be exercised by the Members.

22.2 The Board shall exercise its powers subject to:

- (a) the Act;
- (b) these Articles;
- (c) any shareholders' agreement in force; and
- (d) any duly passed Reserved Matter resolution.

22.3 Each Director owes to the Company the duties set out in the Act, including the duties to act in good faith, to exercise care, skill and diligence, to avoid conflicts of interest, and to declare any interest in proposed transactions.

### 23. Conflicts of Interest

23.1 A Director who is in any way interested (directly or indirectly) in a proposed transaction or arrangement with the Company shall declare the nature and extent of their interest to the other Directors in accordance with the Act.

23.2 Subject to the Act, a Director may participate in the consideration of a matter in which they have an interest, provided that the Director has made the required declaration and the Board (excluding the interested Director) determines that the matter is on arm's length terms and is in the best interests of the Company.

23.3 The Company shall maintain a Register of Directors' Interests in accordance with the Act.

### 24. Remuneration of Directors

24.1 The Directors may be paid such remuneration as the Board (excluding the interested Director) determines, subject to approval by ordinary resolution of the Members.

24.2 The Directors may be paid all reasonable travelling, hotel, and other expenses properly incurred by them in attending and returning from meetings of the Board, general meetings, or otherwise in connection with the business of the Company.

### 25. Borrowing Powers

25.1 The Board may exercise all the powers of the Company to borrow money, give guarantees, grant security over the assets of the Company, and otherwise raise finance, subject to these Articles (including the Reserved Matters) and the Act.

### 26. Board Meetings

26.1 The Board shall meet at least once every calendar quarter, unless otherwise resolved by the Board.

26.2 Notice of every Board meeting shall be given to every Director, in writing or by Electronic Means, at least seven (7) days in advance, unless all Directors consent to shorter notice.

26.3 The quorum at a Board meeting is two (2) Directors present in person or by Electronic Means, or such other number as the Board may determine (provided it is not less than two).

26.4 Questions arising at a Board meeting shall be decided by a majority of votes. In the case of an equality of votes, the chairperson shall have a second or casting vote.

26.5 A Director may participate in a Board meeting by Electronic Means, provided that all participants can communicate with each other.

26.6 A resolution in writing signed or approved by Electronic Means by all Directors is as valid as if it had been passed at a Board meeting duly convened and held.

### 27. Chairperson

27.1 The Directors may appoint one of their number to be chairperson of the Board. The chairperson shall preside at all Board meetings and general meetings at which they are present.

27.2 If the chairperson is not present at a meeting within fifteen (15) minutes after the time appointed, the Directors present shall appoint one of their number to chair the meeting.

### 28. Secretary

28.1 The Board may appoint a Secretary for such term, at such remuneration, and on such conditions as they think fit. The Secretary may be a Director.

28.2 The Secretary shall be responsible for the maintenance of statutory registers, the filing of statutory returns, the convening of meetings, the keeping of minutes, and such other duties as the Board may determine.

## PART V — ACCOUNTS, AUDIT, AND DIVIDENDS

### 29. Accounting Records

29.1 The Directors shall ensure that accounting records are kept in accordance with the Act. The accounting records shall be kept at the registered office or at such other place as the Directors think fit, and shall at all times be open to inspection by the Directors.

29.2 The Directors shall prepare and lay before the Members in general meeting such accounts and reports as are required by the Act.

### 30. Audit

30.1 The Company shall appoint an Auditor in accordance with the Act, except where the Company is exempt from the requirement to appoint an Auditor under the Act.

### 31. Dividends

31.1 The Company may, by ordinary resolution, declare dividends, but no dividend shall exceed the amount recommended by the Board.

31.2 Subject to the Act, the Board may pay interim dividends if justified by the profits of the Company.

31.3 All dividends shall be paid to the holders of Ordinary Shares in proportion to the number of Ordinary Shares held by them.

31.4 No dividend shall bear interest against the Company.

### 32. Reserves

32.1 The Board may, before recommending any dividend, set aside out of the profits of the Company such sums as it thinks proper as a reserve or reserves to be employed in the business of the Company.

## PART VI — NOTICES, INDEMNITY, AND MISCELLANEOUS

### 33. Notices

33.1 Any notice to be given to or by any person under these Articles shall be in writing, except where these Articles or the Act permit otherwise.

33.2 A notice may be given by the Company to any Member:

- (a) by delivering it personally;
- (b) by sending it by post or courier to the Member's address in the Register of Members;
- (c) by Electronic Means to the email address provided by the Member; or
- (d) by any other means permitted by the Act.

33.3 A notice is deemed served:

- (a) if delivered personally, at the time of delivery;
- (b) if sent by post, forty-eight (48) hours after posting;
- (c) if sent by Electronic Means, at the time of transmission, provided no delivery failure notice is received; and
- (d) in any other case, at the time it would reasonably be expected to be received.

### 34. Indemnity

34.1 Subject to the Act, the Company may indemnify any Director, Secretary, or other officer of the Company against any liability incurred by them in defending any proceedings (whether civil or criminal) in which judgment is given in their favour or in which they are acquitted, or in connection with any application in which relief is granted to them by a court.

34.2 The Company may purchase and maintain insurance for the benefit of any Director, Secretary, or officer in respect of any liability which may lawfully be insured against.

### 35. Winding Up

35.1 Subject to the Act, the Company may be wound up by special resolution of the Members.

35.2 On a winding up, the assets of the Company available for distribution among the Members shall, subject to the rights attaching to any class of shares, be applied in paying to the Members pro rata according to the number of Ordinary Shares held.

### 36. Execution of Documents

36.1 Documents executed by the Company may be signed by:

- (a) any two Directors; or
- (b) one Director and the Secretary; or
- (c) any person or persons authorised by a resolution of the Board or the Members.

36.2 The Company may, but is not obliged to, have a common seal. If the Company has a common seal, the seal shall only be used with the authority of the Board, and every instrument to which the seal is affixed shall be signed by one Director and countersigned by the Secretary or a second Director.

### 37. Compliance with Data Protection

37.1 The Company shall process personal data (including the personal data of Members, Directors, officers, and employees) only in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019, the Data Protection Regulations, and any guidance issued by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

37.2 The Company shall ensure that the statutory registers maintained under these Articles are made available for inspection in a manner consistent with the Data Protection Act, 2019.

### 38. Amendment of Articles

38.1 These Articles may only be amended by special resolution of the Members in accordance with the Act.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

The following items require verification by a licensed Kenyan Advocate before execution:

1. **Compatibility with the Act.** Each clause in these Articles must be cross-checked against the current text of the Companies Act, 2015. The Act contains mandatory provisions that cannot be displaced even by unanimous Member agreement (e.g. directors' duties under Part X; certain audit thresholds).
2. **Reserved Matters thresholds.** Article 19 lists thresholds recommended for early-stage private companies but should be reviewed against the Company's expected transaction volume and any future shareholders' agreement.
3. **Pre-emption rights.** Article 11 should be reviewed against any future shareholders' agreement to ensure the two are consistent.
4. **Founder protections.** The Articles deliberately do not include weighted voting or super-voting classes. If dual-class voting is later desired for investor protection, it must be added at the relevant time and must be compatible with private-company restrictions under the Act.
5. **Indemnity.** Article 34 is subject to the limits in the Act on indemnities to Directors. The Advocate must confirm the limits currently in force.
6. **Data protection.** Article 37 references the Data Protection Act, 2019. The Company will also need a separate Data Protection Policy, Privacy Policy, and (where applicable) Data Processing Agreements — these are not contained in the Articles.
7. **Beneficial Ownership.** Article 14.2 refers to the BOI Regulations. The Company must maintain a Register of Beneficial Owners and comply with current BRS filing requirements; the Advocate should confirm the current procedural mechanics.
8. **Execution formalities.** The Advocate should confirm whether wet-ink signatures, electronic signatures, or a combination are acceptable for BRS filing in 2026.
