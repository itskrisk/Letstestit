### 4. Objects

The objects for which the Company is established are:

- (a) To design, build, operate, maintain, license and commercialise digital software platforms, marketplace infrastructure, logistics technology, routing and dispatch systems, payment and settlement integrations, mobile and web applications, APIs, and related software products and services.
- (b) To operate, facilitate, support and manage local commerce, last-mile delivery, merchant networks, courier and fleet operations, and associated technology and infrastructure services.
- (c) To carry on any other lawful trade, business, or service that is ancillary to, complementary with, or naturally connected to the objects set out above, including the acquisition and disposal of any property, rights, or interests that may be necessary or convenient for the attainment of those objects.
- (d) To do all such other things as are incidental or conducive to the attainment of the above objects or any of them.

> **Drafting note:** the objects clause is drafted broadly so as to accommodate the full scope of Muncheez's business model — including its technology, marketplace, logistics, payments integration, and SaaS activities — without restricting the Company to food delivery. ## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Memorandum vs. Statement in Lieu.** The Advocate should confirm the current BRS practice as to whether the Memorandum is required to be filed in full, or whether a Statement in Lieu is sufficient in conjunction with the Articles.
2. **Witness.** Kenyan incorporation practice requires witnesses on subscriber signatures. The Advocate should confirm whether a Commissioner for Oaths, an Advocate, or another prescribed person is acceptable.
3. **Objects clause.** The objects clause is drafted broadly to accommodate the full scope of Muncheez's technology and logistics platform. The Advocate should confirm this is consistent with current BRS practice and does not trigger any specific regulatory threshold.
4. **Subscription totals.** The number of shares each subscriber takes must reconcile with the Statement of Capital filed with CR1 and the initial Register of Members. All three documents must be consistent.
5. **ID / KRA PIN formatting.** Ensure the format of identification numbers matches BRS KYC requirements at the date of filing.
