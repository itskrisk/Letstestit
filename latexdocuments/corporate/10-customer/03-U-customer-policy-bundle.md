## 1. REFUND POLICY

### 1.1 When a refund is available

A refund may be available where:

- (a) the Order is incorrect, damaged, incomplete, or of unacceptable quality;
- (b) the Order does not match the product listing;
- (c) the Order has not been delivered within a reasonable time;
- (d) the Customer cancelled before preparation commenced;
- (e) any other circumstance set out in the Customer Terms of Service.

### 1.2 When a refund is not available

A refund is not available where:

- (a) the Customer simply changed their mind after preparation commenced and the Merchant (in its reasonable discretion) declines cancellation;
- (b) the Customer provided an incorrect or incomplete delivery address and the Order was delivered to that address;
- (c) the Customer was unavailable at the delivery address and the Order could not be delivered;
- (d) the Customer refused delivery without valid reason;
- (e) the Order was consumed or substantially consumed after delivery.

### 1.3 How to request a refund

Refunds must be requested through the Platform within **24 hours** of receipt of the Order (or within **48 hours** of the expected delivery time if the Order was not delivered).

### 1.4 Refund processing

- (a) Muncheez reviews the request and may contact the Customer, the Merchant, or the Rider for information.
- (b) Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within **[INSERT — recommend 5–10 business days]**.
- (c) Muncheez may, at its discretion, issue refunds as Platform credit in lieu of monetary refund, where the Customer agrees.

### 1.5 Partial refunds

Partial refunds may be issued where only part of the Order is affected.

## 2. CANCELLATION POLICY

### 2.1 Customer cancellation

- (a) The Customer may cancel an Order **before preparation has commenced** through the Platform.
- (b) Once preparation has commenced, cancellation is at the Merchant's reasonable discretion. The Merchant may charge a cancellation fee to cover the cost of prepared items.
- (c) If the Order has already been dispatched, cancellation may not be possible. The Customer may refuse delivery but may be charged a delivery fee.

### 2.2 Merchant cancellation

The Merchant may cancel an Order where:

- (a) a Product is out of stock;
- (b) the Merchant cannot prepare the Order within the stated time;
- (c) the Order fails any fraud or compliance check;
- (d) the Customer's instructions are ambiguous or unsafe.

### 2.3 Muncheez cancellation

Muncheez may cancel an Order where:

- (a) the Order fails a fraud or compliance check;
- (b) there is a Platform issue preventing fulfilment;
- (c) the cancellation is required by Applicable Law.

### 2.4 Refunds on cancellation

Refunds for cancelled Orders are processed in accordance with Section 1 (Refund Policy).

## 3. DELIVERY POLICY

### 3.1 Delivery area

Muncheez delivers within designated operating areas as published on the Platform. Delivery outside these areas may not be available.

### 3.2 Delivery times

- (a) Estimated delivery times are indicative, not guaranteed.
- (b) Actual delivery times depend on merchant preparation time, traffic, weather, and Rider availability.
- (c) Muncheez will use reasonable efforts to communicate any significant delays to the Customer.

### 3.3 Delivery address

- (a) The Customer is responsible for providing an accurate and complete delivery address.
- (b) The Customer must be available (or have an authorised person available) at the delivery address to receive the Order.
- (c) If the Customer is unavailable, the Rider may attempt to contact the Customer via the in-app chat or phone. If the Customer remains unavailable after a reasonable time, the Rider may return the Order to the Merchant and the Order may be subject to a cancellation fee.

### 3.4 Delivery handover

- (a) The Order is handed over to the Customer (or the authorised person) at the delivery address.
- (b) The Rider may request identification where required (e.g. for age-restricted products).
- (c) Proof of delivery may be obtained via photo, signature, or in-app confirmation.

### 3.5 Delivery fees

- (a) Delivery fees are displayed before the Customer confirms payment.
- (b) Delivery fees may vary based on distance, demand, and other factors.
- (c) Free delivery promotions may be available from time to time.

### 3.6 Delivery issues

- (a) Damaged or incorrect Orders must be reported within **24 hours** of receipt.
- (b) Missing items must be reported within **24 hours** of receipt.
- (c) Muncheez will investigate and resolve the issue in accordance with the Refund Policy.

### 3.7 Food safety and allergens

- (a) Food Products are prepared by the Merchant. Muncheez is not responsible for the accuracy of allergen information or for food-safety compliance at the Merchant's premises.
- (b) Customers with allergies or dietary requirements must contact the Merchant directly before ordering.

## 4. PROMOTIONS TERMS

### 4.1 Eligibility

Promotions are available to eligible Customers as set out in the specific promotion terms.

### 4.2 Specific promotion terms

Each promotion has its own terms, which are presented at the point of offer. Specific terms prevail over these general terms to the extent of any inconsistency.

### 4.3 Abuse and fraud

Muncheez may revoke a promotion or reward if it reasonably suspects:

- (a) fraud or manipulation;
- (b) breach of the specific promotion terms;
- (c) creation of multiple accounts to obtain multiple rewards;
- (d) any other abuse of the promotion.

### 4.4 Withdrawal

Muncheez may withdraw or modify a promotion at any time, with reasonable notice where appropriate.

## 5. REFERRAL TERMS

### 5.1 Eligibility

Referral rewards are available to Customers who refer new users in accordance with these terms.

### 5.2 Reward structure

The reward for a successful referral is set out in the specific promotion presented to the referrer.

### 5.3 Conditions

A referral is successful where:

- (a) the referred user creates a new account using the referrer's unique link or code;
- (b) the referred user completes the qualifying action (e.g. first Order of a minimum value);
- (c) the referrer and the referred user comply with all applicable terms.

### 5.4 Restrictions

- (a) Self-referrals are not permitted.
- (b) Referrals may not be combined with other promotions.
- (c) Muncheez may cap the number of referrals or the total reward per referrer.

### 5.5 Tax

Referral rewards may be subject to tax. Muncheez may withhold tax as required by law.

## 6. CUSTOMER COMPLAINT PROCEDURE

### 6.1 How to make a complaint

- (a) Complaints may be made through the Platform, by email to **support@muncheez.com**, or by telephone to **[INSERT TELEPHONE]**.
- (b) Complaints should include the Order reference, the Customer's contact details, and a description of the issue.

### 6.2 Acknowledgement

Muncheez will acknowledge the complaint within **two (2) business days** of receipt.

### 6.3 Investigation

Muncheez will investigate the complaint, which may involve contacting the Customer, the Merchant, the Rider, or third parties. Muncheez may request supporting evidence.

### 6.4 Resolution

Muncheez will use reasonable efforts to resolve the complaint within **fifteen (15) business days**. The resolution may include:

- (a) a refund;
- (b) a replacement Order;
- (c) Platform credit;
- (d) an apology and explanation;
- (e) any other remedy Muncheez considers appropriate.

### 6.5 Escalation

If the Customer is not satisfied with the resolution, the Customer may escalate the complaint to the relevant regulator (e.g. the Competition Authority of Kenya under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012).

### 6.6 Record-keeping

Muncheez will maintain a record of all complaints, in accordance with its Data Retention Policy and Applicable Law.
