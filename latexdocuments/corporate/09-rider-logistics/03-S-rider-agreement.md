## ⚠️⚠️ CRITICAL DRAFTING NOTE — READ FIRST ⚠️⚠️

This document is **dual-purpose**. The classification of the Rider under the **Employment Act, 2007** depends on the **actual working relationship** between Muncheez and the Rider — not on what the contract calls them. The Advocate / HR must perform a classification analysis **before** this Agreement is signed by any Rider.

Two sub-templates are provided:

- **Sub-template A — Independent Contractor Rider Agreement** — for use where the relationship is genuinely one of independent contractor (the Rider is free to accept or reject dispatches, sets their own hours, uses their own vehicle, is free to provide services to competitors, and bears commercial risk).
- **Sub-template B — Employee Rider Agreement** — for use where the relationship is that of an employee (Muncheez controls the working hours, provides the vehicle, prohibits other work, and bears the commercial risk).

If classification is uncertain, **default to the more protective arrangement for the Rider** (typically the employee sub-template) until clarified by the Advocate.

The body of this Agreement below is the **common provisions** that apply in either case. The **schedules differ** depending on the sub-template chosen.

## DOCUMENT PURPOSE

Sets the contractual framework between Muncheez and each Rider who performs delivery and dispatch services on the Platform. Allocates responsibilities for vehicles, equipment, expenses, insurance, location data, safety, and incident response.

## LEGAL BASIS

- **Employment Act, 2007** — primary source for the employee / contractor classification test and statutory protections (where applicable).
- **Traffic Act (Cap. 403)** — driver licensing, vehicle registration, road traffic rules.
- **Insurance Act (Cap. 487)** — third-party motor vehicle insurance (mandatory).
- **Data Protection Act, 2019** — location data and rider personal data.
- **Consumer Protection Act, 2012** — to the extent the Rider interacts with Customers.
- **Public Health Act and county by-laws** — for food handling (where applicable).

## WHO USES IT

- **Muncheez** — relies on it for the relationship framework with each Rider.
- **Riders** — sign it before performing any dispatch.
- **Court / Tribunal** — uses it to determine the actual nature of the relationship.

## COMMON PROVISIONS

### 1. Definitions

- **"Agreement"** — this Rider Agreement, including the applicable Schedule (A or B).
- **"Customer"** — a person who has placed an order through the Platform.
- **"Dispatch"** — a single assignment to pick up an Order from a Merchant and deliver it to a Customer.
- **"Order"** — a Customer's order placed through the Platform.
- **"Platform"** — the Muncheez platform.
- **"Rider"** — the person entering into this Agreement with Muncheez.
- **"Services"** — the dispatch and delivery services performed by the Rider under this Agreement.
- **"Vehicle"** — the means of transport used by the Rider (foot, bicycle, motorcycle, scooter, car, or other) as notified to Muncheez.

### 2. Engagement

2.1 Muncheez engages the Rider, and the Rider accepts the engagement, to perform the Services on the terms set out in this Agreement and the applicable Schedule.

2.2 The applicable Schedule is **Schedule [A / B]**, as determined by the classification analysis at onboarding. The classification may be revisited if the working relationship materially changes.

### 3. Onboarding and Verification

3.1 The Rider must complete Muncheez's onboarding process, including KYC, vehicle documentation (where applicable), and training.

3.2 The Rider must hold (where applicable):

- (a) a valid driving licence for the Vehicle class;
- (b) valid vehicle registration;
- (c) valid third-party motor vehicle insurance (mandatory under the Traffic Act);
- (d) any other licence, permit, or registration required by Applicable Law for the operation of the Vehicle (including county transport permits where required).

3.3 The Rider must notify Muncheez immediately if any of the above expire, are suspended, or are revoked.

### 4. Performance of the Services

4.1 The Rider agrees to perform the Services with reasonable care, skill, and diligence, and in compliance with Applicable Law (including the Traffic Act, food-handling requirements, and any county by-laws).

4.2 The Rider must:

- (a) maintain a professional appearance and use any branded equipment provided;
- (b) handle Orders with care and comply with any food-handling or hygiene requirements;
- (c) follow the dispatch instructions communicated through the Rider app, except where doing so would be unsafe or unlawful;
- (d) communicate with Customers in a professional and respectful manner;
- (e) deliver Orders to the address provided and obtain proof of delivery where required;
- (f) report any incident (accident, dispute, complaint, damage, delay) to Muncheez promptly.

4.3 The Rider must not:

- (a) consume alcohol or any intoxicating substance while performing the Services;
- (b) carry any unauthorised passenger or item unrelated to the Dispatch;
- (c) solicit Customers for any purpose outside the Platform;
- (d) discriminate against Customers on any protected ground.

### 5. Vehicle, Equipment, and Expenses

5.1 The Rider is responsible for providing the Vehicle and any equipment not provided by Muncheez.

5.2 Muncheez may, at its discretion, provide certain equipment (e.g. an insulated delivery bag, branded high-visibility gear, an ID badge). Any such equipment remains the property of Muncheez and must be returned on termination.

5.3 The Rider is responsible for all Vehicle-related expenses, including fuel, maintenance, insurance, and licensing, except where this Agreement or the applicable Schedule expressly provides otherwise.

### 6. Earnings, Fees, and Payment

6.1 The Rider's earnings are calculated in accordance with the applicable Schedule and the rate card in force from time to time.

6.2 Muncheez shall pay the Rider's earnings by M-Pesa or bank transfer to the Rider's designated account, in accordance with the applicable Schedule.

6.3 The Rider is responsible for all taxes on the Rider's earnings. Muncheez may withhold any taxes it is required to withhold by law.

### 7. Location Data

7.1 The Rider acknowledges that, to perform the Services, Muncheez needs to collect the Rider's location data while the Rider app is in active use.

7.2 The Rider consents to the collection and processing of location data for in:

- (a) matching dispatches to nearby Riders;
- (b) tracking the progress of a Dispatch for Customer support;
- (c) safety and incident response;
- (d) compliance with Applicable Law.

7.3 The Rider may disable location sharing through the device settings, but doing so will prevent the Rider from receiving Dispatches.

7.4 Location data is processed in accordance with the **Privacy Policy** and the **Data Protection Act, 2019**.

### 8. Personal Data

8.1 Muncheez processes the Rider's personal data in accordance with the **Privacy Policy** and the **Data Protection Act, 2019**.

8.2 The Rider processes personal data of Customers only for the purpose of performing the Dispatch and shall not retain, use, or disclose such personal data for any other purpose.

### 9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Rider shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of Muncheez, its Merchants, and its Customers.

9.2 The obligation survives termination for **two (2) years** (or longer for trade secrets).

### 10. Safety and Incident Response

10.1 The Rider must report any accident, injury, dispute, or complaint to Muncheez within **twenty-four (24) hours**.

10.2 Muncheez will provide **24/7 Fleet Dispatch Support** through the Rider app.

10.3 The Rider must comply with all applicable health and safety requirements, including any COVID-19 or other public-health guidance communicated by Muncheez or the relevant authority.

### 11. Insurance

11.1 The Rider must maintain third-party motor vehicle insurance covering the Vehicle throughout the term of this Agreement.

11.2 The Rider is encouraged to maintain additional insurance covering personal accident, theft, and damage to the Vehicle.

11.3 Muncheez may, at its discretion, maintain contingent insurance covering accidents involving Riders on Dispatch. The existence and scope of any such insurance will be communicated to the Rider.

### 12. Conduct and Disciplinary

12.1 The Rider must comply with Muncheez's **Rider Code of Conduct**.

12.2 Material breach of this Agreement, the Code of Conduct, or Applicable Law may result in suspension, deactivation, or termination of the Rider's account.

### 13. Suspension and Termination

13.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **fourteen (14) days'** written notice (or such longer notice as required by the applicable Schedule).

13.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if:

- (a) the other Party commits a material breach that is not cured within **seven (7) days** of written notice;
- (b) the other Party becomes insolvent or enters liquidation;
- (c) the other Party is convicted of a criminal offence involving dishonesty, fraud, or violence;
- (d) continuing the Agreement would breach Applicable Law.

13.3 Muncheez may suspend the Rider's access to the Platform pending investigation of any alleged breach, safety incident, or fraud.

13.4 On termination:

- (a) the Rider's access to the Platform is revoked;
- (b) Muncheez settles any amounts owed to the Rider for completed Dispatches, less any amounts owed by the Rider to Muncheez;
- (c) the Rider returns any Muncheez property in their possession;
- (d) clauses that by their nature survive (including clauses 7, 8, 9, 14, 15) continue in force.

### 14. Limitation of Liability

14.1 Subject to Applicable Law, neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

14.2 The Rider's aggregate liability to Muncheez under this Agreement is capped at the total earnings paid by Muncheez to the Rider in the **twelve (12) months** preceding the event giving rise to the claim, except in the case of fraud or wilful misconduct.

### 15. Dispute Resolution

15.1 The Parties shall use good-faith efforts to resolve any dispute through senior-management discussion within **fourteen (14) days**.

15.2 Failing settlement, the dispute shall be referred to mediation administered by the **Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch)**, and failing settlement, to binding arbitration in Nairobi under the **Arbitration Act (Cap. 49 of the Laws of Kenya)**, **except** where the dispute falls within the jurisdiction of the Employment and Labour Relations Court (in which case the Court has jurisdiction).

### 16. General

16.1 **Governing law:** the laws of the Republic of Kenya.
16.2 **Notices:** notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, registered post, or email to the addresses set out above.
16.3 **Variation:** no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
16.4 **Severability:** if any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
16.5 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter.
16.6 **Counterparts:** this Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by Electronic Means.

## SCHEDULE A — INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RIDER

> **Use this Schedule where the Rider is genuinely an independent contractor.**

### A1. Nature of the Relationship

A1.1 The Rider is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Muncheez.

A1.2 Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship. The Rider is responsible for their own taxes, insurance, vehicle, and equipment.

A1.3 The Rider is free to provide services to other platforms or customers, subject to Clause A1.5 (non-solicit of Muncheez Customers during the term).

A1.4 The Rider is free to accept or reject any Dispatch offered through the Platform.

A1.5 During the term of this Agreement, the Rider shall not solicit any Customer of Muncheez for the purpose of providing services outside the Platform.

### A2. Earnings

A2.1 The Rider is paid per completed Dispatch, at the rate set out in the rate card communicated by Muncheez from time to time.

A2.2 Earnings are exclusive of VAT (if applicable). Muncheez may issue a VAT invoice to the Rider where required.

A2.3 Muncheez shall pay earnings **weekly** in arrears to the Rider's designated M-Pesa or bank account.

### A3. Hours and Availability

A3.1 The Rider may set their own hours by toggling their status to "Online" or "Offline" in the Rider app.

A3.2 The Rider is not required to be online at any particular time.

### A4. Vehicle and Equipment

A4.1 The Rider is responsible for providing and maintaining the Vehicle and any equipment not provided by Muncheez.

A4.2 Muncheez may, at its discretion, lend branded equipment (e.g. delivery bag, ID badge, high-visibility vest). Any such equipment remains the property of Muncheez and must be returned on termination.

### A5. Taxes and Statutory Obligations

A5.1 The Rider is responsible for all taxes on their earnings, including income tax, VAT (if applicable), and any other taxes.

A5.2 Muncheez may withhold any taxes it is required to withhold by law (e.g. withholding tax on management fees where applicable).

A5.3 The Rider is responsible for compliance with all Applicable Law relating to the operation of the Vehicle, including traffic and licensing requirements.

### A6. Insurance

A6.1 The Rider must maintain:

- (a) valid third-party motor vehicle insurance covering the Vehicle (mandatory);
- (b) any other insurance required by Applicable Law;
- (c) personal accident insurance (recommended).

A6.2 Muncheez does not provide workers' compensation or similar insurance to the Rider. The Rider acknowledges that they bear the commercial risk of performing the Services.

### A7. Termination

A7.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **fourteen (14) days'** written notice.

### A8. Governing Law of Engagement

A8.1 The relationship between the Parties is governed by the contract law of Kenya and the principles of independent contractor relationships, not by the Employment Act, 2007.

## SCHEDULE B — EMPLOYEE RIDER

> **Use this Schedule where the Rider is genuinely an employee of Muncheez. This Schedule incorporates the statutory protections of the Employment Act, 2007.**

### B1. Nature of the Relationship

B1.1 The Rider is an employee of Muncheez.

B1.2 The terms of employment are set out in this Schedule and in the **Employee Handbook**, the **Disciplinary Policy**, the **Leave Policy**, and any other applicable policy.

B1.3 The Rider's employment is subject to the **Employment Act, 2007** and any other Applicable Law.

### B2. Probation

B2.1 The first **three (3) months** of employment are probationary. Either Party may terminate the employment during probation on **seven (7) days'** written notice (or such shorter notice as required by the Employment Act, 2007).

### B3. Remuneration

B3.1 The Rider's remuneration is set out in the **letter of appointment**.

B3.2 Remuneration shall be paid **monthly**, no later than the **5th** business day of the following month, by bank transfer or M-Pesa.

B3.3 Deductions from remuneration shall be limited to those permitted by the Employment Act, 2007 (including PAYE, NHIF, NSSF, and any court-ordered deductions).

### B4. Hours of Work

B4.1 The Rider's normal hours of work shall be as scheduled by Muncheez, subject to the Employment Act, 2007.

B4.2 Overtime shall be paid in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007.

### B5. Leave

B5.1 The Rider is entitled to annual leave, sick leave, and other statutory leave in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007 and the **Leave Policy**.

B5.2 Leave requests shall be made through the Rider app or the HR system.

### B6. Vehicle and Equipment

B6.1 Muncheez may provide a Vehicle for the Rider's use during working hours. The Rider must take reasonable care of the Vehicle and report any damage or malfunction immediately.

B6.2 Muncheez shall provide all equipment necessary for the performance of the Services.

B6.3 Muncheez shall maintain the Vehicle (where provided) and bear the costs of fuel, insurance, and licensing, except for damage caused by the Rider's wilful misconduct or negligence.

### B7. Statutory Obligations

B7.1 Muncheez shall register the Rider with KRA, NHIF, and NSSF and shall remit the relevant contributions.

B7.2 Muncheez shall maintain workers' compensation insurance and any other insurance required by Applicable Law.

B7.3 Muncheez shall issue a Certificate of Service on termination, in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007.

### B8. Disciplinary Procedure

B8.1 Any disciplinary action shall be taken in accordance with the **Disciplinary Policy** and the procedural fairness requirements of the Employment Act, 2007, s. 41.

### B9. Termination

B9.1 Either Party may terminate the employment in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007.

B9.2 Notice periods are as set out in the Employment Act, 2007 (typically **one month's** notice or pay in lieu).

B9.3 Summary dismissal is reserved for the causes set out in the Employment Act, 2007 (gross misconduct, etc.) and shall follow the procedure in the Disciplinary Policy.

### B10. Restraint

B10.1 During employment, the Rider shall not engage in any activity that competes with Muncheez.

B10.2 Post-employment restraint is subject to the limits in the Employment Act, 2007, s. 33. Any post-employment restraint shall be reasonable in scope, duration, and geography.

### B11. Governing Law of Engagement

B11.1 The relationship between the Parties is governed by the **Employment Act, 2007** and other Applicable Law.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS — CRITICAL

1. **Classification analysis.** ⚠️ **This is the most important legal issue in this document.** Muncheez must perform an honest classification analysis before signing any Rider. Misclassification carries Employment Act exposure (back-pay of wages, benefits, statutory deductions, severance, and potentially penalties). The Advocate should review the classification criteria.
2. **Schedule selection.** If classification is mixed (some riders genuinely independent, others genuinely employees), the Advocate should advise on whether to operate a single Schedule or two distinct Schedules.
3. **Vehicle arrangement.** The Schedule selection may depend in part on who provides the vehicle. If Muncheez provides the vehicle, the relationship is more likely to be characterised as employment.
4. **Restraint.** Schedule B includes a post-employment restraint. Such restraints are subject to the Employment Act, 2007, s. 33. The Advocate should confirm the restraint is reasonable.
5. **Insurance.** The Advocate should review the mandatory insurance requirements against current Kenyan law, particularly the Insurance Act and the Traffic Act.
6. **County permits.** Some counties require transport permits for delivery riders. Muncheez should verify the requirements in each operating county.
