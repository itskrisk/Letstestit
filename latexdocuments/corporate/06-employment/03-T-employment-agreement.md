## 1. **Position and Duties**

1.1 The Employee is employed as **[INSERT JOB TITLE]**, reporting to **[INSERT SUPERVISOR / MANAGER]**.

1.2 The Employee's duties are set out in the **job description** attached as **Schedule 1**.

1.3 The Employee shall perform their duties faithfully, diligently, and to the best of their ability, and shall act at all times in the best interests of the Employer.

1.4 The Employee shall comply with the Employer's policies, including the **Employee Handbook**, the **Disciplinary Policy**, the **Leave Policy**, the **Acceptable Use Policy**, the **Company Device Policy**, and the **Data Protection Policy**, each as amended from time to time.

## 2. **Commencement and Term**

2.1 The Employee's employment commences on **[INSERT START DATE]** (the "Start Date") and continues until terminated in accordance with this Agreement or the Employment Act, 2007.

2.2 The first **[INSERT NUMBER]** months of employment are **probationary**. During probation, either Party may terminate this Agreement on **seven (7) days'** written notice (or such shorter notice as required by the Employment Act, 2007).

## 3. **Place of Work**

3.1 The Employee's normal place of work is **[INSERT OFFICE ADDRESS / "Remote within Kenya"]**.

3.2 The Employer may require the Employee to travel on business from time to time.

## 4. **Hours of Work**

4.1 The Employee's normal hours of work are **[INSERT HOURS, e.g. 09:00–17:00, Monday to Friday]**.

4.2 The Employee may be required to work additional hours as necessary for the performance of their duties, subject to the Employment Act, 2007. Overtime shall be paid or compensated in accordance with the Act.

## 5. **Remuneration**

5.1 The Employee shall be paid a **gross monthly salary of KES [INSERT AMOUNT]**, payable by bank transfer to the Employee's nominated account no later than the **[INSERT DATE, e.g. 5th]** business day of the following month.

5.2 The salary shall be reviewed annually in accordance with the Employer's compensation review process. The Employer does not guarantee an increase.

5.3 The Employee may be eligible for discretionary bonuses, benefits, and allowances as set out in **Schedule 2**.

## 6. **Deductions**

6.1 Deductions from salary shall be limited to those permitted by the Employment Act, 2007, including PAYE, NHIF, NSSF, and any court-ordered deductions.

6.2 No other deductions may be made without the Employee's written consent.

## 7. **Leave**

7.1 The Employee is entitled to annual leave, sick leave, maternity/paternity leave, and other statutory leave in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007 and the **Leave Policy**.

7.2 Leave requests must be made through the Employer's HR system and approved by the Employee's manager.

## 8. **Pension and Statutory Benefits**

8.1 The Employer shall register the Employee with KRA, NHIF, and NSSF and shall remit the relevant employer contributions. The Employee's contributions shall be deducted from salary.

## 9. **Confidentiality**

9.1 The Employee shall keep confidential all Confidential Information of the Employer, its Merchants, its Riders, its Customers, and its business partners.

9.2 This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

9.3 Confidential Information includes source code, algorithms, business plans, financial information, customer data, merchant data, rider data, partner terms, internal policies, and any other information identified as confidential or that a reasonable person would treat as confidential.

## 10. **Intellectual Property**

10.1 The Employee discloses and assigns absolutely to the Employer, with full title guarantee, all of their present and future right, title, and interest (including all intellectual property rights) in and to any work, invention, design, software, code, content, or other material created, developed, or contributed by the Employee in the course of their employment or in connection with the Employer's business.

10.2 The Employee waives, in favour of the Employer, all moral rights in respect of such work to the extent permitted by Applicable Law.

10.3 The Employee undertakes to execute such documents and do such further acts as the Employer may reasonably require to perfect the assignment in Clause 10.1.

## 11. **Data Protection**

11.1 The Employee shall comply with the **Data Protection Act, 2019** and the Employer's **Data Protection Policy** and **Privacy Policy** in respect of any personal data they process in the course of their employment.

11.2 The Employee's personal data is processed in accordance with the Employer's **Employee Privacy Notice** (attached as **Schedule 3**).

## 12. **Acceptable Use**

12.1 The Employee shall comply with the **Acceptable Use Policy** in respect of the Employer's systems, devices, networks, and data.

12.2 The Employee shall not use the Employer's systems for any unlawful purpose or for personal purposes unrelated to the Employer's business.

## 13. **Company Devices**

13.1 Where the Employer provides the Employee with a device (e.g. laptop, phone), the Employee shall comply with the **Company Device Policy**, take reasonable care of the device, and return it on termination.

## 14. **Conflict of Interest**

14.1 The Employee shall promptly disclose to the Employer any actual or potential conflict of interest.

14.2 The Employee shall not, without the Employer's prior written consent, be engaged or interested in any business that competes with the Employer.

## 15. **Restraint During Employment**

15.1 During employment, the Employee shall not engage in any activity that competes with the Employer or that interferes with the performance of their duties.

## 16. **Post-Employment Restraint**

16.1 Subject to the limits in the Employment Act, 2007, s. 33, the Employee agrees that for a period of **[INSERT PERIOD, e.g. six (6) months]** following termination of employment, they shall not, without the Employer's prior written consent:

- (a) solicit any Customer, Merchant, or Rider of the Employer with whom they had material contact during the last twelve (12) months of employment; or
- (b) solicit any employee of the Employer with whom they had material contact during the last twelve (12) months of employment.

16.2 The Employee acknowledges that this restraint is reasonable in scope, duration, and geography for the protection of the Employer's legitimate business interests.

16.3 If any court holds this restraint unenforceable, the Parties intend for the restraint to be read down to the maximum extent enforceable under Applicable Law.

## 17. Termination

17.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007.

17.2 After probation, the Employer may terminate the Agreement:

- (a) on **one (1) month's** written notice, or pay in lieu of notice; or
- (b) summarily, for the causes set out in the Employment Act, 2007 (gross misconduct), following the procedure in the **Disciplinary Policy**.

17.3 The Employee may terminate the Agreement on **one (1) month's** written notice (or such longer notice as required by the Act).

17.4 On termination, the Employee shall:

- (a) return all Employer property, including devices, documents, and access tokens;
- (b) settle any outstanding amounts;
- (c) cooperate in the handover of duties;
- (d) continue to comply with Clauses 9, 10, 11, 16.

17.5 The Employer shall issue a Certificate of Service in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007.

## 18. Disciplinary and Grievance

18.1 Any disciplinary action shall be taken in accordance with the **Disciplinary Policy** and the procedural fairness requirements of the Employment Act, 2007, s. 41.

18.2 Grievances shall be handled in accordance with the Employer's grievance procedure.

## 19. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

19.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya.

19.2 The Parties shall attempt to resolve any dispute through the Employer's grievance procedure.

19.3 Failing settlement, any dispute relating to or arising out of this employment relationship shall be referred to the **Employment and Labour Relations Court** in accordance with the Employment Act, 2007 and the Labour Institutions and Processes Act (where applicable).

## 20. General

20.1 **Notices:** notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, registered post, or email.
20.2 **Variation:** no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
20.3 **Severability:** if any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
20.4 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement (together with the Schedules and the Employee Handbook) constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter.
20.5 **Counterparts:** this Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by Electronic Means.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Written particulars.** This Agreement is intended to satisfy the **Employment Act, 2007, s. 9** written-particulars requirement. The Advocate should confirm that all required particulars (including hours, place of work, remuneration, leave, notice period, and grievance procedure) are covered.
2. **Probation.** The Employment Act permits a probationary period of up to six (6) months for non-managerial employees. The Advocate should confirm the appropriate probation length.
3. **Notice period.** The Employment Act sets out minimum notice periods based on length of service. The Agreement sets one (1) month after probation, which should be confirmed against the Act.
4. **Restraint.** Clause 16 is subject to the Employment Act, 2007, s. 33. Post-employment restraints are enforceable in Kenya but must be reasonable. The Advocate should confirm the restraint is reasonable in scope, duration, and geography.
5. **Disciplinary procedure.** The Disciplinary Policy must comply with the procedural fairness requirements of the Employment Act, 2007, s. 41.
6. **Certificate of Service.** Clause 17.5 references a Certificate of Service. The Advocate should confirm the form and timing.
