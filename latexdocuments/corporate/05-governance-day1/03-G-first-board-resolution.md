- **Companies Act, 2015** — Part VIII (directors) and the duty to keep minutes of Board meetings.

## WHO USES IT

- **Directors** — adopt the resolutions.
- **Company Secretary** — minute the meeting, circulate, and file the signed copy.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **First Board Meeting.** This template assumes the first Board meeting is held within days of incorporation. The Advocate should confirm the timing and any notice requirements under the Articles.
2. **Auditor appointment.** Clause 3.3 contemplates both an audit-required and an audit-exempt scenario. The Advocate should confirm the current audit threshold under the Act.
3. **Rider classification.** Clause 9.2 flags the classification issue. The Advocate should not allow the Rider Agreement template to be used until this is decided.
4. **Bank mandate.** The Bank will prescribe its own mandate form. Clause 5 should be reviewed against the Bank's actual mandate.
