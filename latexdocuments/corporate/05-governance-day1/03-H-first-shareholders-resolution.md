Records the formal decisions of the Shareholders at their first meeting, including ratification of Director actions, appointment of Aud, allotment of shares, adoption of constitutional and governance documents, and other foundational matters.

## WHO USES IT

## RESOLUTIONS

At a meeting of the Shareholders of **Muncheez Technologies Limited** (the "Company") duly convened and held on **[INSERT DATE]** at **[INSERT LOCATION / ELECTRONIC PLATFORM]**, the Shareholders present being **Dean Ndere** and **Kris Kamau** (together holding 100% of the issued share capital of the Company), the following resolutions were duly passed:

### 1. Chair and Quorum

1.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that **Dean Ndere** be appointed as Chairperson of the meeting.

1.2 **IT WAS NOTED** that all Shareholders were present (in person or by proxy or by Electronic Means) and that the meeting was duly convened.

### 2. Ratification of Director Actions

2.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that all actions taken by the Directors of the Company between the date of incorporation and the date of this meeting, as set out in the First Board Resolution, be and are hereby ratified, confirmed and approved.

### 3. Appointment of Auditor

3.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that, where the Company is required by the Act to appoint an Auditor, **[INSERT AUDITOR FIRM NAME]** be and is hereby appointed as Auditor of the Company, to hold office until the conclusion of the next Annual General Meeting (or until such time as the Shareholders resolve otherwise), at a fee to be agreed.

3.2 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that, where the Company is exempt from the audit requirement under the Act, the Directors may obtain an independent review of the accounts in lieu of an audit.

### 4. Adoption of Constitutional and Governance Documents

4.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that the Company adopts, with effect from the date of this resolution, the following documents:

- (a) the Articles of Association (as adopted at incorporation);
- (b) the Founders' Agreement;
- (c) the Shareholders' Agreement;
- (d) the First Board Resolution.

### 5. Confirmation of Allotment

5.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that the allotment of shares by the Directors to the subscribers as recorded in the First Board Resolution be and is hereby confirmed, and that the entries in the Register of Members be and are hereby ratified.

### 6. Authorisation of Officers

6.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that the Directors be and are hereby authorised to appoint any officer of the Company on such terms as the Directors may determine, subject to the Articles and the Reserved Matters in the Shareholders' Agreement.

### 7. Pre-emption Waiver

7.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that, for the avoidance of doubt, the pre-emption rights in the Articles and the Shareholders' Agreement are waived in respect of any allotment of Shares to a Director, officer, employee, or consultant of the Company pursuant to any employee share scheme adopted by the Board.

### 8. General Authority

8.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that the Directors be and are hereby authorised to do all such acts and execute all such documents as may be necessary or expedient to give effect to these resolutions.

### 9. Close

9.1 **IT WAS RESOLVED** that, there being no further business, the meeting be closed.
