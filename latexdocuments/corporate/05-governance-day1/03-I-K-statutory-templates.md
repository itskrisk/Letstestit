## 03-M — STATUTORY REGISTER MAINTENANCE POLICY

### Purpose

Internal policy for the maintenance of the statutory registers and the making of statutory filings.

### Legal Basis

Companies Act, 2015; BOI Regulations.

### Policy

1. The Company Secretary is responsible for the maintenance of all statutory registers of the Company.
2. Each register must be kept at the registered office of the Company, or at such other location as the Board may from time to time resolve.
3. Each register must be made available for inspection by Members, Directors, the Auditor, and any competent regulatory authority in accordance with the Act.
4. The Company Secretary must ensure that each entry in a register is supported by the underlying document (e.g. an allotment resolution, a transfer instrument, a director's disclosure form).
5. The Company Secretary must file with BRS, within the periods prescribed by the Act and the BOI Regulations:
   - (a) annual returns;
   - (b) beneficial ownership updates;
   - (c) any change in directors, members, registered office, or share capital;
   - (d) any return of allotment;
   - (e) any other filing required by the Act, the BOI Regulations, or any other applicable law.
6. The Company Secretary must prepare a Statutory Filings Calendar at the start of each calendar year and circulate it to the Board.
7. The Board must review the statutory registers at each quarterly Board meeting.
8. Personal data in the statutory registers must be processed in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Company's Privacy Policy.

## FOUNDER INPUTS REQUIRED (across all six templates)

- All `[TO BE CONFIRMED]` and `[INSERT ...]` placeholders.
- Share allotment figures (must reconcile with the Memorandum, Articles and Register of Members).
- Choice of bank and Authorised Signatory schedule.
- Internal thresholds in 03-K (cheque limits, transfer limits) and 03-N (Reserved Matter Schedule).

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Share certificate.** Confirm that the format complies with the Act. Some companies include additional particulars (e.g. a notice about the Articles) which the Advocate may recommend.
2. **Bank mandate.** The Bank's actual mandate form will prevail. The template above is a Company-side authorisation that supports the Bank form, not a substitute for it.
3. **Statutory registers.** The Company Secretary should confirm the exact fields required by each register against the Act and the BOI Regulations. The fields above are common practice but not necessarily exhaustive.
4. **Reserved Matter thresholds.** The thresholds in 03-K and 03-N are recommended defaults. The Advocate and the Board should calibrate them to the Company's expected transaction volume.
