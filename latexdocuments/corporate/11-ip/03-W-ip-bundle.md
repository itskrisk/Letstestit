This bundle consolidates the core intellectual-property and confidentiality documents. Each sub-document is a drafting template. They are **not** legal advice and must be reviewed by a licensed Kenyan Advocate before execution.

### Recitals

(A) Prior to incorporation, the Assignor developed the Muncheez. platform source code, algorithms, database designs, architecture, technical documentation, and associated intellectual property (the **"Pre-Existing IP"**).

(B) The Parties wish to record the assignment of the Pre-Existing IP and of all future improvements to the Company.

### Operative Terms

#### 1. Definitions

- **"Pre-Existing IP"** — all intellectual property developed by the Assignor prior to incorporation in connection with the Muncheez. platform, including source code, algorithms, database designs, architecture, technical documentation, designs, and trade secrets.
- **"Improvements"** — all modifications, enhancements, derivative works, and other intellectual property developed by the Assignor after incorporation in connection with the Company's business.
- **"IP Rights"** — all intellectual property rights, including copyrights, patents (where applicable), trademarks, design rights, database rights, trade secrets, and rights in confidential information.

#### 2. Assignment

2.1 The Assignor hereby assigns absolutely to the Company, with full title guarantee and free from all encumbrances, all of their right, title, and interest (including all IP Rights) in and to the Pre-Existing IP.

2.2 The Assignor hereby assigns absolutely to the Company, with full title guarantee, all future Improvements developed by the Assignor during the term of their engagement with the Company, with effect from the date each Improvement comes into existence.

#### 3. Moral Rights

3.1 To the extent permitted by Applicable Law, the Assignor waives all moral rights in respect of the Pre-Existing IP and the Improvements in favour of the Company.

#### 4. Further Assurance

4.1 The Assignor undertakes to execute such documents and do such further acts as the Company may reasonably require to perfect the assignments in Clause 2.

#### 5. Warranties

5.1 The Assignor warrants that:

- (a) they are the sole owner of the Pre-Existing IP;
- (b) the Pre-Existing IP does not infringe any third-party rights;
- (c) the Pre-Existing IP is not subject to any encumbrance, licence, or restriction;
- (d) they have not granted any rights in the Pre-Existing IP to any third party.

#### 6. Consideration

6.1 The Parties acknowledge that the consideration for this assignment is the issue of shares to the Assignor as a subscriber at incorporation and the engagement of the Assignor by the Company as Chief Technology Officer.

#### 7. Governing Law

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya.

#### 8. Dispute Resolution

8.1 Any dispute shall be referred to mediation administered by the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch), and failing settlement, to binding arbitration in Nairobi under the Arbitration Act (Cap. 49 of the Laws of Kenya).

## 5. OPEN-SOURCE SOFTWARE POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the rules for using, contributing to, and distributing open-source software ("OSS") within the Company's technology stack.

### Policy

1. **Inventory.** The CTO shall maintain an OSS Register listing every OSS component used by the Company, including the component name, version, licence, source, and any modifications made by the Company.

3. **Licence compatibility.** Before incorporating any OSS into the Company's products, the CTO shall confirm that the OSS licence is compatible with the Company's distribution model (proprietary SaaS, mobile app distribution, etc.) and with the OSS already in use.

3. **Copyleft.** Where the Company uses copyleft OSS (e.g. GPL, AGPL), the CTO shall assess the implications for proprietary code and document the assessment.

4. **Attribution.** The Company shall comply with all OSS attribution requirements, including notices in the application, documentation, or "About" screens.

5. **Contributions.** Employees may contribute to OSS projects, subject to the CTO's approval, where the contribution does not contain proprietary Company code and does not conflict with the Company's interests.

6. **Prohibited OSS.** The CTO shall maintain a list of OSS components that may not be used (e.g. components with security vulnerabilities, components with licences incompatible with the Company's products).

7. **Audit.** The CTO shall audit the OSS Register at least annually and on each major release.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Assignment scope.** Clause 2.1 of the Founder IP Assignment Agreement uses "all IP Rights" — the Advocate should confirm this is broad enough to capture copyright, database rights, design rights, trade secrets, and any patentable elements.
2. **Moral rights.** Clause 3.1 of the Founder IP Assignment Agreement waives moral rights to the extent permitted by Applicable Law. Under Kenyan copyright law, moral rights are recognised in some forms. The Advocate should confirm the enforceability of the waiver.
3. **Trademarks.** The IP Register references KIPI trademark applications. The Advocate should advise on classification (NICE classes) and the timing of the applications.
4. **Domains.** The Domain Register must reflect the actual registered domains. The Advocate should advise on defensive registrations (e.g. `muncheez-ke.com`, common misspellings).
5. **OSS Policy.** The OSS Policy is internal. The Advocate should review for compliance with the licences actually used in the Company's stack (a software audit may be needed to confirm the OSS inventory).
6. **Brand guidelines.** The full Brand Guidelines document is referenced but not drafted here. The Marketing team should draft it; the Advocate should review.
