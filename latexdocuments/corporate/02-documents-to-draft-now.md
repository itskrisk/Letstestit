## TIER 1 — DRAFT BEFORE / AT INCORPORATION

These documents must be in draft form *before* the CR1 filing is submitted.

### Document 03-A — Articles of Association (Kenyan private company limited by shares)

- **Document Purpose:** the constitutional document of the company. It governs internal affairs: share rights, director powers, shareholder meetings, dividends, transfers, winding-up, etc.
- **Legal Basis:** Companies Act, 2015, ss. 25–31 and Schedule (Model Articles). Kenya permits the use of Model Articles unmodified, modified, or fully bespoke Articles.
- **Who Uses It:** signed by all subscribers at incorporation; amended by special resolution later.
- **Information Required Before Finalisation:**
  - Authorised share capital (KES)
  - Nominal value per share (or no par value decision)
  - Class structure (single class vs. multiple classes — keep simple for now unless investor planning)
  - Any pre-emption rights variations
  - Any reserved matters beyond the statutory defaults
- **Founder Inputs Required:** capital structure decisions above.
- **Legal Review Flags:** whether to adopt Model Articles unmodified, modify them, or draft bespoke; whether founder protections (e.g. weighted voting) are compatible with private-company restrictions under the Act.
- **Status:** `[TO BE DRAFTED — BY ADVOCATE]`

### Document 03-B — Memorandum of Association (or Statement in Lieu)

- **Document Purpose:** historically, the company's foundational document; under the 2015 Act, much of its role has been subsumed into the Articles, but a Memorandum is still filed in many cases.
- **Legal Basis:** Companies Act, 2015, s. 22 (and any current BRS requirement).
- **Who Uses It:** signed by subscribers at incorporation.
- **Information Required:** subscriber names, addresses, ID, number of shares each subscriber takes.
- **Founder Inputs Required:** subscriber list (initially the founders).
- **Legal Review Flags:** whether the company will file a full Memorandum or rely on a Statement in Lieu of Memorandum under s. 22 — depends on Articles drafting and BRS current practice.
- **Status:** `[TO BE DRAFTED — BY ADVOCATE]`

### Document 03-C — CR1 Application (Pre-populated Draft)

- **Document Purpose:** the application form filed with BRS for incorporation.
- **Legal Basis:** Companies Act, 2015; BRS Form CR1.
- **Who Uses It:** submitted by subscribers / agent via BRS eCitizen.
- **Information Required:** every founder input on the Master Register (registered office, directors, subscribers, share capital, etc.).
- **Status:** `[TO BE PRE-POPULATED — BY ADVOCATE]`

### Document 03-D — Beneficial Ownership Information (Pre-populated Draft)

- **Document Purpose:** identifies every beneficial owner of the company for the BOI register.
- **Legal Basis:** Companies (Beneficial Ownership Information) Regulations; Companies Act, 2015.
- **Who Uses It:** filed at incorporation and updated on changes.
- **Information Required:** every beneficial owner (typically the founders, plus any person controlling ≥ statutory threshold) — full name, ID, residential address, ownership chain.
- **Status:** `[TO BE PREPARED — BY ADVOCATE]`

### Document 03-E — Founders' Agreement

- **Document Purpose:** sets out the founders' mutual relationship: roles, responsibilities, equity, vesting, departure, IP, confidentiality, decision-making, dispute resolution.
- **Legal Basis:** contract law (between the founders as individuals) + Kenya law.
- **Who Uses It:** signed by both founders.
- **Information Required:**
  - Founder equity split — `[FOUNDER EQUITY TO BE CONFIRMED]`
  - Vesting schedule (e.g. 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff — common but not mandatory)
  - Good leaver / bad leaver definitions
  - Founder roles (CEO / CTO split)
  - Decision-making framework (unanimous vs. majority)
- **Founder Inputs Required:** equity percentages, vesting preference, bad-leaver triggers.
- **Legal Review Flags:** restraint-of-trade provisions must respect Employment Act, 2007, s. 33; non-compete during employment is more enforceable than post-employment.
- **Status:** `[TO BE DRAFTED — once equity confirmed]`

### Document 03-F — Shareholders' Agreement

- **Document Purpose:** governs ongoing shareholder relations: voting, reserved matters, share transfers, pre-emption, tag-along, drag-along, deadlock, exit. Designed to be amendable when investors enter.
- **Legal Basis:** contract law; Companies Act, 2015 (must not conflict with statute).
- **Who Uses It:** all current and future shareholders.
- **Information Required:** equity split, reserved matters list, transfer restrictions, deadlock mechanism.
- **Status:** `[TO BE DRAFTED — once equity confirmed]`

## Drafting Workflow Per Document

For every document I draft, the output will include the following seven sections:

1. **DOCUMENT PURPOSE** — what it does.
2. **LEGAL BASIS** — the actual Kenyan legal basis (Act / Regulation / official guidance).
3. **WHO USES IT** — who signs, approves, maintains.
4. **INFORMATION REQUIRED** — every input I need before finalisation.
5. **DRAFT** — the complete professional document, with placeholders for missing information.
6. **FOUNDER INPUTS REQUIRED** — list of unresolved placeholders.
7. **LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS** — only genuine issues requiring Advocate / Company Secretary review.

## Sequencing Principle

The company should never operate without:
- properly drafted constitutional documents (Articles, Founder agreements);
- properly drafted employment / contractor / service-provider agreements for anyone working for the company;
- a published Privacy Policy before processing any personal data;
- signed merchant / rider agreements before onboarding;
- proper statutory registers maintained.

Drafting in advance of the trigger is always better than drafting under pressure after the trigger.
