## 1. Introduction

**Muncheez Technologies Limited** ("Muncheez", "we", "us", "our") is a Kenyan technology and logistics company that operates a digital platform connecting customers, merchants, and riders. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you interact with our website, applications, services, or any of our business operations.

We take your privacy seriously. We process personal data only in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of the Laws of Kenya and any other applicable law.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Personal data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **"Processing"** means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, or deletion.
- **"Data subject"** means the natural person to whom personal data relates.
- **"Services"** means the Muncheez platform, applications, websites, and any related services we provide.

## 4. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

### Customers
- Identification data (name, email, phone number)
- Delivery data (delivery addresses, GPS location with your consent)
- Order data (order history, preferences)
- Payment-related data (handled via licensed payment service providers — we do not store full card numbers)
- Communications data (support tickets, in-app messages)
- Device and usage data (IP address, device identifiers, app version, language)
- Marketing preferences and consent

### Merchants
- Identification and KYC data (name, business name, ID/Passport, KRA PIN, business address, bank / M-Pesa settlement details)
- Catalogue data (product listings, prices, descriptions)
- Order, settlement, and tax data
- Communications data

### Riders
- Identification and KYC data
- Vehicle / mode-of-transport data
- Location data (where the rider app is in use, subject to consent)
- Trip data (pick-up, drop-off, route, completion time)
- Earnings and settlement data
- Performance and safety data (ratings, complaints)

### Employees, Contractors, Consultants, and Applicants
- Identification and contact data
- KYC, KRA PIN, NHIF/NSSF data (where applicable)
- Employment or engagement records
- Performance and disciplinary records

### Website and App Visitors
- Device and usage data (IP address, browser type, pages visited)
- Cookie data (see our Cookie Notice)

## 5. Sources of Personal Data

We collect personal data:

- directly from you, when you register, place an order, list a product, sign up as a rider, apply for a job, or contact us;
- automatically, through your use of our website and apps (e.g. device data, location data with consent);
- from third parties, including KRA, payment service providers, KYC / identity verification vendors, and (where lawful) public sources.

## 6. Purposes and Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal data for the following purposes, on the lawful bases set out below:

| Purpose | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Providing the Muncheez platform and its features | Performance of a contract with you |
| Processing orders, dispatching deliveries, settling payments | Performance of a contract; legal obligation (tax, anti-money-laundering) |
| Verifying merchants, riders, and other counterparties | Legal obligation; legitimate interest in fraud prevention |
| Customer service and complaint handling | Performance of a contract; legitimate interest |
| Marketing communications to you (subject to consent) | Consent (which you can withdraw at any time) |
| Service analytics and product improvement | Legitimate interest (with appropriate safeguards) |
| Compliance with legal and regulatory obligations (KRA, ODPC, BRS) | Legal obligation |
| Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims | Legal obligation; legitimate interest |
| Safety, security, fraud detection, and incident response | Legal obligation; legitimate interest; vital interests (in extreme cases) |

If we rely on consent, we will tell you at the point of collection and you may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

## 7. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell personal data. We share personal data only with:

- **Service providers** who act as our processors and are bound by written Data Processing Agreements (e.g. cloud hosting, payment service providers, KYC vendors, email and SMS aggregators, courier operations vendors);
- **Merchants and riders** to the extent necessary to fulfil an order (e.g. a merchant sees the customer's delivery address and contact details for the order; a rider sees the customer's delivery address and contact details for a delivery);
- **Other users** to the extent necessary for the platform to function;
- **Regulators and competent authorities** where required by law or in response to a valid legal request;
- **Professional advisers** (lawyers, auditors) bound by an equivalent duty of confidentiality;
- **A buyer or investor** in connection with a corporate transaction (with appropriate safeguards).

If we transfer personal data outside Kenya, we will ensure that the recipient is bound by adequate safeguards as required by the DPA and the ODPC's guidance on cross-border transfers.

## 8. Location Data

Where we collect precise GPS / location data (for example from the rider app or the customer app), we do so only:

- with your consent (which you can withdraw at any time, subject to platform functionality);
- for the limited purposes set out at the point of collection (e.g. dispatch, delivery tracking, safety);
- and only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

You can disable precise location sharing through your device settings, but doing so may prevent certain features of the Services from working.

## 9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies on our websites and apps. Please see our **Cookie Notice** for details of the cookies we use, their purposes, and how to manage your preferences.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, or as required by law. Typical retention periods include:

| Category | Retention |
|---|---|
| Customer account data | For the duration of the account, plus a reasonable period for queries and disputes (typically 6 years from last activity) |
| Order, settlement, and tax records | As required by KRA / tax law (typically 7 years from end of relevant fiscal year) |
| Merchant and rider KYC | For the duration of the relationship plus 7 years |
| Marketing consent | Until you withdraw consent |
| Logs and security data | Typically 12–24 months unless required to be kept longer |
| Employee / contractor records | As required by employment law and tax law |

When personal data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise it.

## 11. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls, encryption in transit and at rest, logging and monitoring, and staff training. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach occurs, we will follow our **Data Breach Response Policy** and, where required, notify the ODPC and affected data subjects.

## 12. Data Subject Rights

Subject to the DPA and any applicable exceptions, you have the right to:

- be informed about how we process your personal data (this Privacy Policy);
- access your personal data;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
- request deletion of your personal data;
- object to processing for direct marketing, or for purposes based on legitimate interest;
- restrict or object to certain types of processing;
- data portability, where applicable;
- not be subject to automated decision-making that significantly affects you (we do not currently engage in such automated decision-making — see the Data Protection Policy);
- withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent;
- lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our DPO at **privacy@muncheez.com**. We will respond within the period prescribed by the DPA.

## 13. Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have collected personal data from a child, please contact us at **privacy@muncheez.com** so we can delete it.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date below will reflect the latest version. Material changes will be communicated through the platform or by email where appropriate.

## 15. Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we process your personal data, please contact our DPO first at **privacy@muncheez.com**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner**.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **ODPC registration.** This Policy does not replace the requirement to register with the ODPC as a Data Controller / Processor. The Advocate should also review the ODPC's current registration portal data fields so the Policy aligns with what is disclosed to ODPC.
2. **Cross-border transfers.** Clause 7 references cross-border transfers. If Muncheez uses cloud providers outside Kenya, the Advocate should review the safeguards in place.
3. **Automated decision-making.** Clause 12 states we do not currently engage in automated decision-making that significantly affects data subjects. If Muncheez's algorithms (dispatch, ranking, fraud detection) constitute such processing, the Policy must be amended and a separate DPIA may be required.
4. **Children.** Clause 13 sets the threshold at 18. The Advocate should confirm the age-of-majority rule under Kenyan law (currently 18) and consider whether Muncheez wishes to allow 16–17-year-olds to use the platform with parental consent (the DPA contemplates processing of children's data with verifiable parental consent in some contexts).
5. **Retention periods.** The retention table in Clause 10 reflects common practice. The Advocate should confirm against current KRA / tax / employment law retention requirements.
