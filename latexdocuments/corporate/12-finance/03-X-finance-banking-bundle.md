## 3. FINANCIAL CONTROLS POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the internal controls over financial transactions, segregation of duties, and reporting.

### Policy

1. **Segregation of duties.** No single person may initiate, approve, and execute a financial transaction. All payments require at least one initiator and one approver.
2. **Bank access.** Online banking credentials are held by the CFO (or in their absence, the CEO) and the CTO (as a backup). Passwords are rotated quarterly.
3. **Reconciliations.** Bank reconciliations are performed monthly by the CFO (or external accountant) and reviewed by the CEO.
4. **Cash management.** Cash floats are minimised. Receipts and payments are made through bank channels wherever possible.
5. **Reporting.** Monthly management accounts are produced within twenty-one (21) days of month-end and circulated to the Board.
6. **Audit.** Where the Company meets the audit threshold, audited annual accounts are prepared. Where exempt, accounts are independently reviewed.
7. **Anti-fraud.** All suspected fraud is reported immediately to the Board. The Board may engage external forensic accountants where appropriate.
8. **Insurance.** The Company maintains appropriate insurance cover (fidelity, cyber, professional indemnity) where commercially prudent.

## 4. PROCUREMENT POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the rules for procuring goods and services.

### Policy

1. **Thresholds.**
   - Below KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 50,000]: any single Director may approve.
   - KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 50,000–250,000]: any two Directors.
   - Above KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 250,000]: Board approval.
2. **Vendor selection.** Vendors are selected on a transparent basis, with at least three quotes where practicable.
3. **Conflict of interest.** Directors and employees must declare any conflict of interest in respect of a procurement and must not participate in the approval.
4. **Contracts.** Any procurement with a contract value above KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 100,000] must be supported by a written contract reviewed by the Company Secretary or Advocate.
5. **Records.** All procurement records (quotes, purchase orders, invoices, contracts) are retained for at least seven (7) years.

## 5. EXPENSE POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the rules for incurring and reimbursing expenses.

### Policy

1. **Eligible expenses.** Reasonable business expenses incurred in the course of duties (travel, meals, accommodation, conference fees, software subscriptions, equipment) are reimbursable.
2. **Approval.** Expenses are approved by the relevant manager in accordance with the Payment Approval Matrix.
3. **Receipts.** Receipts must be retained for all expenses. Expenses without receipts may be declined.
4. **Personal expenses.** Personal expenses are not reimbursable.
5. **Cash advances.** Cash advances are discouraged. Where used, they must be reconciled within fourteen (14) days.
6. **Reimbursement timeline.** Approved expenses are reimbursed within seven (7) business days of approval.

## 7. PETTY CASH POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the rules for operating a petty cash float.

### Policy

1. **Float.** The petty cash float shall not exceed KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 50,000].
2. **Custodian.** The petty cash is held by the Office Manager (or equivalent).
3. **Limit per transaction.** No single petty cash transaction may exceed KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 10,000].
4. **Receipts.** All petty cash disbursements must be supported by a receipt.
5. **Replenishment.** The float is replenished when it falls below KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 10,000]. Replenishment requires the same approvals as a payment of the equivalent value.
6. **Audit.** The petty cash is reconciled monthly and audited annually.

## 8. RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTION POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the rules for transactions between the Company and any Director, Shareholder, Founder, officer, or any person connected with any of them.

### Policy

1. **Definition.** A related-party transaction includes any transaction between the Company and any Director, Shareholder, Founder, officer, or any person connected with any of them.
2. **Disclosure.** The interested Director, Shareholder, Founder, or officer must disclose the nature and extent of their interest to the Board as soon as practicable.
3. **Approval.** Related-party transactions above KES [TO BE CONFIRMED — recommend 50,000] require Board approval. The interested Director abstains from the vote.
4. **Fairness.** Related-party transactions must be on arm's length terms and in the best interests of the Company.
5. **Recording.** Related-party transactions are recorded in the minutes of the Board and disclosed in the annual accounts.
6. **Reserved Matters.** Related-party transactions above the thresholds in the Articles and the Shareholders' Agreement require Supermajority Consent.

## 9. ACCOUNTING RECORDS POLICY

### Purpose

Sets out the rules for maintaining accounting records in compliance with the Companies Act, 2015, Part XV, and applicable tax law.

### Policy

1. **Books.** The Company maintains proper accounting records sufficient to show and explain all transactions, the financial position of the Company, and to enable the preparation of accurate financial statements.
2. **Location.** Records are kept at the registered office or at such other place as the Board decides.
3. **Retention.** Records are retained for at least seven (7) years from the end of the relevant financial year, or longer where required by tax, employment, or other Applicable Law.
4. **Currency.** The Company's functional and reporting currency is KES. Transactions in other currencies are translated at the spot rate on the transaction date, with gains and losses recorded in the income statement.
5. **Accounting framework.** Accounts are prepared in accordance with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) or such other framework as the Board may determine, having regard to the size of the Company and any future investor requirements.
6. **Accounting period.** The Company's financial year ends on [INSERT DATE — e.g. 31 December], unless the Board resolves otherwise.
7. **Access.** Directors and the Auditor have full access to the accounting records at all times.

## 10. INVOICE PROCEDURES

### Purpose

Sets out the procedures for issuing and receiving invoices.

### Policy

1. **Outgoing invoices.** All sales invoices are issued through the Company's accounting system (or the platform's invoicing module) and include:
   - (a) the Company's name, address, KRA PIN, and (where applicable) VAT number;
   - (b) the customer's name and address;
   - (c) a unique invoice number;
   - (d) the date of issue;
   - (e) a description of the goods or services;
   - (f) the unit price, quantity, and total;
   - (g) the applicable VAT and any other taxes.
2. **eTIMS compliance.** Invoices are issued in compliance with the current eTIMS regulations. The Advocate should confirm the current requirements.
3. **Incoming invoices.** All incoming invoices are recorded in the accounting system within seven (7) days of receipt.
4. **Approval.** Incoming invoices are approved by the relevant manager in accordance with the Payment Approval Matrix.
5. **Credit notes.** Credit notes are issued in the same format as invoices and clearly marked as credit notes.
