## ⚠️ Drafting note

This Merchant Agreement is a drafting template intended to be entered into between Muncheez and each merchant on the platform. It is **not** legal advice and must be reviewed by a licensed Kenyan Advocate before use. The Agreement treats the merchant as a **separate principal** — Muncheez is a technology / logistics platform, not the seller of the merchant's goods or the provider of the merchant's services, except where Muncheez explicitly accepts that role (for example, in the dispatch and payment processing layers of the service).

The Agreement is drafted to accommodate merchants who are not restaurants — restaurants, retailers, home-based businesses (where lawfully permitted), shops, and other local commerce vendors.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Applicable Law"** — all laws, regulations, and regulatory guidance applicable to a Party in Kenya, including consumer-protection, food-safety, advertising, data-protection, and tax laws.
- **"Customer"** — any natural person who places an Order through the Platform.
- **"Data Protection Laws"** — the Data Protection Act, 2019 and the Data Protection Regulations, 2021, and any guidance issued by the ODPC.
- **"Order"** — a request by a Customer for Products placed through the Platform.
- **"Platform"** — the Muncheez website, mobile applications, and related technology.
- **"Products"** — the goods and services the Merchant lists on the Platform.
- **"Rider"** — an independent logistics service provider or employee authorised to perform dispatch and delivery on the Platform.
- **"Settlement"** — the payment by Muncheez to the Merchant for the value of Orders fulfilled, net of Commissions, fees, refunds, and other deductions.

## 2. Onboarding and Verification

2.1 The Merchant must complete Muncheez's onboarding process before listing any Products on the Platform. Onboarding includes the Merchant KYC / Due Diligence Form and the Settlement Information Form.

2.2 The Merchant must provide true, complete, and accurate information during onboarding. The Merchant must promptly notify Muncheez of any material change to that information.

2.3 Muncheez may refuse or suspend onboarding if it reasonably suspects fraud, money-laundering, breach of food-safety or other Applicable Law, or any other risk to the Platform or its users.

## 3. Merchant Representations and Warranties

3.1 The Merchant represents and warrants that:

- (a) it has the legal capacity to enter into this Agreement and to sell the Products;
- (b) it holds all licences, permits, and registrations required by Applicable Law for the sale of the Products (including, where applicable, food-handler permits, county business permits, and KRA requirements);
- (c) the Products comply with Applicable Law, including food-safety, allergen-disclosure, labelling, weights-and-measures, advertising, and consumer-protection requirements;
- (d) the Products do not infringe any third-party intellectual property or other rights;
- (e) it will comply with Data Protection Laws in respect of any personal data it receives from Muncheez (e.g. customer contact details for delivery); and
- (f) it is not subject to any sanctions, criminal investigation, or insolvency proceeding that would materially impair its ability to perform this Agreement.

## 4. Catalogue, Pricing, and Availability

4.1 The Merchant must submit a complete and accurate catalogue of Products through the Merchant onboarding form or the merchant dashboard. The catalogue must include accurate descriptions, prices, allergens (where applicable), images, and availability.

4.2 The Merchant is responsible for setting its own prices. Muncheez may from time to time recommend or display prices but is not responsible for the Merchant's pricing decisions.

4.3 The Merchant must keep the catalogue up to date, including prices, availability, opening hours, and stock. The Merchant must promptly remove a Product from the catalogue when it is no longer available.

4.4 Muncheez may, in its discretion, highlight, rank, or curate Products on the Platform. The Parties acknowledge that curation is part of Muncheez's value proposition and is exercised in good faith.

## 5. Orders, Acceptance, Preparation, and Dispatch

5.1 An Order is placed when the Customer submits a request through the Platform and the Merchant accepts the Order (whether expressly, by implication, or by commencing preparation).

5.2 The Merchant must:

- (a) accept or reject each Order promptly (and within any time limit set by the Platform);
- (b) prepare each accepted Order with reasonable care, in accordance with the Customer's specifications and Applicable Law;
- (c) package the Products securely and in compliance with any packaging or hygiene requirements communicated by Muncheez;
- (d) hand over the Order to the Rider (or, where applicable, make the Order available for pickup) within the time communicated to the Customer.

5.3 The Merchant may reject an Order where:

- (a) a Product is out of stock;
- (b) the delivery address is outside the Merchant's operating area;
- (c) the Order fails any fraud or compliance check;
- (d) the Customer's instructions are ambiguous, unsafe, or unlawful.

The Merchant must not reject Orders on discriminatory grounds.

## 6. Delivery, Cancellations, and Refunds

6.1 Delivery is performed by Riders arranged through the Platform. Muncheez is responsible for dispatching Riders, but the Merchant remains responsible for the preparation, packaging, and accuracy of the Order.

6.2 The Customer may cancel an Order before preparation has commenced. After preparation has commenced, cancellation may be subject to the Merchant's reasonable discretion and Muncheez's published cancellation policy.

6.3 Refunds and customer complaints are administered in accordance with Muncheez's published Refund Policy and Complaint Procedure. Where a complaint relates to the quality, accuracy, or safety of the Products, the Merchant is responsible and Muncheez may pass through any refund to the Customer against the Merchant's settlement.

6.4 The Merchant must participate in good faith in the resolution of customer complaints, including by providing evidence, accepting returns where required, and issuing replacements where appropriate.

## 7. Commissions, Fees, and Settlement

7.1 The Merchant shall pay Muncheez a commission on the gross value of each Order, at the rate set out in **Schedule 1** (the **"Commission"**).

7.2 Muncheez may charge additional fees (e.g. payment-processing fees, marketing fees, subscription fees) at the rates set out in Schedule 1 or as otherwise agreed in writing.

7.3 Muncheez shall settle the net amount owed to the Merchant in accordance with **Schedule 1**, by transfer to the Merchant's designated bank account or M-Pesa number, less:

- (a) the Commission;
- (b) any fees;
- (c) any refunds, chargebacks, or adjustments for Customer complaints attributable to the Merchant;
- (d) any amounts owed by the Merchant to Muncheez.

7.4 The Merchant shall pay all applicable taxes. Muncheez may withhold any taxes it is required to withhold by law.

7.5 Muncheez's settlement statements are final unless the Merchant disputes them in writing within fourteen (14) days of receipt.

## 8. Customer Data

8.1 The Merchant shall process personal data of Customers (e.g. name, delivery address, contact details) only for the purpose of fulfilling Orders and resolving related enquiries, and not for any other purpose without the Customer's separate consent.

8.2 The Merchant shall comply with Data Protection Laws in respect of any personal data it processes. Where Muncheez is the Data Controller and the Merchant is the Data Processor, the Parties shall comply with the **Data Processing Agreement** set out in **Schedule 2**.

8.3 The Merchant shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including secure storage, restricted access, and prompt deletion once the purpose has been fulfilled.

## 9. Intellectual Property

9.1 Each Party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property.

9.2 The Merchant grants Muncheez a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use the Merchant's Product listings, descriptions, images, and trademarks on the Platform and in Muncheez's marketing for the duration of this Agreement. The Merchant may revoke this licence on termination of this Agreement, save that Muncheez may continue to use cached or stored versions to the extent required for operational reasons.

9.3 Muncheez grants the Merchant a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to use Muncheez's name, logo, and platform marks on the Merchant's premises and materials solely for the purpose of indicating that the Merchant is available on the Platform, in accordance with Muncheez's brand guidelines.

9.4 The Merchant shall not (a) register any domain name, social media handle, or trademark that incorporates the **Muncheez.** mark; (b) use the Muncheez. mark in a way that suggests partnership, endorsement, or affiliation beyond the actual arrangement; or (c) modify or create derivative works of any Muncheez marks.

## 10. Marketing and Promotions

10.1 The Merchant may participate in Muncheez's promotions at the Merchant's discretion. The terms of each promotion are agreed in advance.

10.2 Any marketing or promotional claim made by the Merchant in connection with the Platform must be truthful, substantiated, and compliant with Applicable Law.

## 11. Prohibited Conduct

11.1 The Merchant shall not:

- (a) list or sell any Product that is unlawful, counterfeit, unsafe, or prohibited by Applicable Law;
- (b) engage in any fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading conduct toward Customers or Muncheez;
- (c) manipulate ratings, reviews, or rankings;
- (d) provide false KYC or catalogue information;
- (e) circumvent the Platform's payment system (e.g. by directing Customers to pay outside the Platform);
- (f) discriminate against Customers on any protected ground;
- (g) disparage Muncheez or the Platform.

## 12. Suspension and Termination

12.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience on **thirty (30) days'** written notice.

12.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if:

- (a) the other Party commits a material breach that is not cured within **fourteen (14) days** of written notice;
- (b) the other Party becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, or has a receiver appointed;
- (c) the other Party is convicted of a criminal offence involving dishonesty;
- (d) continuing the Agreement would breach Applicable Law or expose the Party to regulatory sanction.

12.3 Muncheez may suspend the Merchant's access to the Platform:

- (a) pending investigation of any alleged breach of this Agreement, fraud, or safety issue;
- (b) where required by a competent authority;
- (c) where the Merchant's actions pose a risk to Customers, Riders, or the Platform.

12.4 On termination:

- (a) the Merchant's catalogue is removed from the Platform;
- (b) Muncheez settles any amounts owed to the Merchant for completed Orders, less any amounts owed by the Merchant;
- (c) each Party returns or destroys the other Party's Confidential Information;
- (d) clauses that by their nature survive (including clauses 8, 9, 13, 14, 15) continue in force.

## 13. Confidentiality

13.1 Each Party shall keep confidential the other Party's Confidential Information and shall not disclose it except as permitted by this Agreement or required by law.

13.2 The confidentiality obligations survive termination for **three (3) years**.

## 14. Data Protection

14.1 The Parties shall comply with Data Protection Laws. Where Muncheez is the Data Controller and the Merchant is the Data Processor, the **Data Processing Agreement** in Schedule 2 applies.

14.2 The Parties shall promptly notify each other of any personal data breach affecting personal data shared under this Agreement and shall cooperate in the investigation and remediation.

## 15. Liability and Indemnity

15.1 Subject to Applicable Law, neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

15.2 The Merchant indemnifies Muncheez against all claims, losses, and expenses arising from (a) the Merchant's Products; (b) the Merchant's breach of this Agreement; (c) the Merchant's breach of Applicable Law; and (d) the Merchant's infringement of any third-party intellectual property.

15.3 Muncheez indemnifies the Merchant against all claims, losses, and expenses arising from Muncheez's wilful breach of this Agreement or wilful misconduct, subject to the cap in Clause 15.4.

15.4 Muncheez's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at the total Commissions paid by the Merchant in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 16. Dispute Resolution

16.1 The Parties shall use good-faith efforts to resolve any dispute through senior-management discussion within **fourteen (14) days** of written notice of the dispute.

16.2 Failing settlement, the dispute shall be referred to mediation administered by the **Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (Kenya Branch)** under its mediation rules in force at the time.

16.3 Failing mediation, the dispute shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration in Nairobi under the **Arbitration Act (Cap. 49 of the Laws of Kenya)**. The seat shall be Nairobi and the language shall be English.

## 17. General

17.1 **Governing law:** the laws of the Republic of Kenya.
17.2 **Notices:** notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier, registered post, or email to the addresses set out above.
17.3 **Variation:** no variation is effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties.
17.4 **Assignment:** the Merchant may not assign this Agreement without Muncheez's prior written consent. Muncheez may assign this Agreement to an Affiliate or in connection with a Change of Control.
17.5 **Severability:** if any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in full force.
17.6 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes any prior agreement.
17.7 **Counterparts:** this Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by Electronic Means.

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Consumer Protection Act.** The Agreement allocates responsibility between Muncheez and the Merchant in a way that is consistent with the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 (e.g. the Merchant is responsible for the quality and safety of the Products; Muncheez is responsible for the platform and dispatch services). The Advocate should confirm this allocation is consistent with the Act and with any regulations made under it.
2. **Food safety.** Where the Merchant is a food business, additional obligations apply (county permits, food-handler training, allergen disclosure). The Agreement does not exhaust these obligations; the Merchant remains responsible for them.
3. **Tax.** Clause 7.4 places the obligation to pay taxes on the Merchant. Muncheez may have withholding or VAT obligations depending on the settlement structure. The Advocate should review against current KRA guidance (including any eTIMS / withholding-tax requirements).
4. **Data Processing.** The summary DPA in Schedule 2 is intended to be replaced by a fuller DPA when the Merchant handles more sensitive processing or large volumes. The Advocate should advise on the threshold.
5. **Liability cap.** The cap in Clause 15.4 is for Muncheez's liability. The Advocate should consider whether a separate (higher) cap is appropriate for certain claims (e.g. data breach, IP infringement).
6. **Restraint.** The Agreement does not contain a post-termination non-compete in respect of the Merchant, on the basis that such restraints are difficult to enforce in Kenya. The Advocate should advise if a tailored restraint is needed in particular cases.
