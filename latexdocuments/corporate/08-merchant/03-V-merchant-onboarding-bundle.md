## 7. MERCHANT SUSPENSION / TERMINATION PROCEDURE

### A. Suspension (Temporary)

A Merchant may be suspended where:

- (a) the Merchant breaches the Merchant Agreement and the breach is curable;
- (b) there is a pending investigation into fraud, food safety, or other compliance issue;
- (c) required by a competent authority;
- (d) the Merchant's actions pose a risk to Customers, Riders, or the Platform.

Suspension is communicated in writing, with reasons and the steps required for reinstatement.

### B. Termination (Permanent)

A Merchant may be terminated where:

- (a) the breach is material and is not cured within the cure period;
- (b) the Merchant becomes insolvent, enters liquidation, or has a receiver appointed;
- (c) the Merchant is convicted of a criminal offence involving dishonesty;
- (d) continuing the relationship would breach Applicable Law;
- (e) the Merchant's actions cause serious harm to Muncheez's reputation or Customers.

Termination is communicated in writing, with reasons, and is implemented in accordance with the Merchant Agreement.

### C. Off-boarding Steps

- [ ] Account deactivated on Platform
- [ ] Catalogue removed
- [ ] Final settlement calculated and paid (net of any amounts owed)
- [ ] Data returned or destroyed (in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement)
- [ ] Reason and lessons learned documented

## LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS

1. **Data minimisation.** The forms above collect only what is operationally or legally required. The Advocate should review each field to confirm no over-collection.
2. **AML / KYC.** The KYC / Due Diligence Form is modelled on standard Kenyan AML practice. The Advocate should confirm the form meets any current AML obligations applicable to Muncheez (Muncheez is not a regulated financial institution, but it may have obligations as a payment aggregator or partner — see Regulatory Matrix).
3. **PEP / sanctions screening.** PEP and sanctions screening requires access to current sanctions lists. The Advocate should confirm Muncheez's approach (in-house or vendor).
4. **Data Protection.** The forms collect personal data. The Merchant Privacy Notice and the underlying lawful basis must support the collection.
