% --- SECTION 1 ---
\section{1. Scope of Role \& Core Responsibilities}

\subsection{1.1 Role Purpose}
The primary mission of the Executive Assistant is to serve as the operational backbone for leadership, driving execution clarity, tracking deliverables, and maintaining team accountability through platform launch.

\subsection{1.2 Essential Duties}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Action Item Tracking:} Synthesizing executive notes into structured operational tasks; tracking cross-departmental deadlines.
    \item \textbf{Schedule Optimization:} Managing calendar syncs, leadership availability, and internal administrative workflows.
    \item \textbf{Launch Preparation:} Tracking pre-launch milestones, highlighting operational blockers, and ensuring timeline adherence.
    \item \textbf{Documentation Anchor:} Maintaining core tracking sheets, executive records, and administrative internal assets.
\end{itemize}

% --- SECTION 2 ---
\section{2. Work Arrangement \& Operating Hours}

\subsection{2.1 Work Environment}
This position is structured as a **Remote-First** role. The Employee shall perform primary duties using electronic productivity and communication tools designated by the Company.

\subsection{2.2 Schedule \& Availability}
The Employee is expected to maintain regular async availability during core operational hours (9:00 AM -- 5:00 PM EAT, Monday through Friday) with standard response windows for urgent leadership communications.

\subsection{2.3 Physical Coordination}
If in-person strategic planning sessions are required, the Employer will provide advance notice and cover pre-approved travel and logistics costs.

% --- SECTION 3 ---
\section{3. Probation, Remuneration \& Growth Path}

\subsection{3.1 Probationary Evaluation}
The position commences with a **three (3) week probationary period** to evaluate workflow integration, communication efficiency, and task accountability.

\subsection{3.2 Compensation Structure}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Post-Probation Stipend:} Upon successful probation, a monthly baseline stipend of **KES 5,000** will be disbursed via agreed digital channels on or before the 1st of each calendar month.
    \item \textbf{Escalation Milestones:} Compensation and job titles will be re-evaluated and scaled dynamically upon:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Formal corporate incorporation alignment.
        \item Seed investment or institutional funding rounds.
        \item Commercial platform market launch and revenue scaling.
    \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}

% --- SECTION 4 ---
\section{4. Termination, Notice \& Separation}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Probation Phase:} Either party may terminate the engagement during probation with **48 hours written notice**.
    \item \textbf{Post-Probation:} Requires **seven (7) days written notice** or stipend payment in lieu of notice.
    \item \textbf{Immediate Termination:} Reserved for gross misconduct, willful breach of duties, or breach of Confidentiality obligations.
\end{itemize}

\section{Schedule A: Proprietary Non-Disclosure Agreement}

\textit{This Schedule forms an independent, legally binding covenant between Muncheez and the Employee.}

\subsection*{A.1 Definition of Confidential Information}
Confidential Information encompasses all non-public operational data, tech assets, and business plans, including:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Technology \& Architecture:} System design, API integrations, database structures, and platform features.
    \item \textbf{Strategy \& Commercials:} Launch roadmaps, merchant databases, marketing formulas, and expansion plans.
    \item \textbf{Financials \& Operations:} Valuation metrics, budget allocations, meeting recordings, and leadership comms.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{A.2 Restrictions \& Covenants}
The Employee covenants that she shall not export, share, or publish Confidential Information to any third party, nor use proprietary assets for personal commercial ventures outside of official duties.

\subsection*{A.3 Intellectual Property Ownership}
All processes, schedules, tracker systems, and operational documents authored or developed by the Employee in performance of her duties are the sole, exclusive property of Muncheez from creation.

\subsection*{A.4 Governing Law \& Survival}
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya. Non-disclosure obligations survive termination of the employment relationship for a period of **two (2) years**.
