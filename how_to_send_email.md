# How to Send Your Birthday Email (via Company Email)

Since you already have a company email (e.g., info@muncheez.com), here is how you can send these HTML templates directly from your existing email client:

## 1. Using Gmail (Company Workspace)
Gmail doesn't have a "Paste HTML" button, but you can use this "Inspect" trick:
1.  **Open** one of the `.html` files in your browser and **copy** the full code (Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C).
2.  **Compose** a new email in Gmail.
3.  Type a unique word like `REPLACE_ME` in the body.
4.  **Right-click** the word `REPLACE_ME` and select **Inspect**.
5.  In the inspector window, right-click the highlighted code and select **Edit as HTML**.
6.  **Delete** everything in that box and **Paste** your template code.
7.  **Click away** or close the inspector. Your email is now ready!

## 2. Using Outlook (Desktop)
1.  **Compose** a new email.
2.  Go to the **Insert** tab and click **Attach File**.
3.  Select the `.html` template from your computer.
4.  Crucial: Instead of clicking "Insert", click the small **downward arrow** on the Insert button and choose **Insert as Text**.
5.  Outlook will now render the HTML directly in your email body.

## 3. Professional Method (Recommended for Branding)
For the best results (best deliverability and no broken layouts), use a professional service like **Resend** or **Mailchimp**:
1.  Link your company domain (muncheez.com) to the service.
2.  Create a "Broadcast" or "Campaign".
3.  Paste the HTML.
4.  This ensures the email looks perfect on every device, including mobile.
